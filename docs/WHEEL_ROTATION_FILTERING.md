# Wheel rotation quality, 30–300 FPS

16 October 2026. This is graphics work in codex/grid-graphics, not a change to source vehicle dynamics. Owner reports jerky tyre lettering/spokes and explicitly requires good appearance at both30 and300FPS. Four acceptance rates:30/60/144/300, with matched physical wheel speeds, acceleration, braking, reverse and standstill. Test side, chase and oblique views in native4K, preserving silhouette, body occlusion and brake detail. Mathematical or static-image checks alone do not complete this acceptance.

## Separate causes

Whole-car translucent trails are a separate TAA defect. Identical controlled moving-camera captures reproduce the trail with TAA; SMAA/off remove that history trail. Enhanced default is now SMAA Ultra. Correct underlying motion vectors/history remain necessary for future temporal reconstruction/DLSS; see GRAPHICS_DEFECTS.md.

Physical state updates every0.016s. Displaying only the last phase repeats poses above62.5Hz. c31ff9a adds bounded render-time spin prediction through Fixed.overstep, capped at16ms, without a persistent render clock or feedback into physics. It reads signed physical wheel spin at wheel+0x104, whose units/sign are demonstrated by vehicle_propulsion's angle integration into wheel+0x110. It does not subtract wrapped angles or infer wheel rotation from car speed. Pause/reset/failure and finished scripted captures use physical phase. Abrupt changes can still have one-step prediction error; this is extrapolation, not complete body/suspension interpolation.

Even exact continuous phase aliases when sampled into discrete pictures. A rotating texture or spoke can jump, appear stationary or reverse. Exposure filtering must handle this independently of pose smoothing.

## Sidewall exposure experiment

Read-only audit_wheel_uv exports6432 purchased wheel vertices privately. A global affine UV fit is invalid for rims and complete two-sided tyre geometry. Splitting sidewalls by axial side reveals a circular chart about UV(0.5,0.5), with mirrored angular orientation. Worst angular deviation is0.00017rad on the outer side and0.00730rad on the other side, across all four tyres. Evidence: local/graphics/wheel-uv01.tsv, wheel-uv-fit01.json, wheel-sidewall-polar-fit01.json. No original mesh/texture data enters Git.

The opt-in --wheel-shutter MILLISECONDS accepts0..33.333; default0 disables the experiment. The independently authored PBR extension affects only Lacetti Tyre Wall meshes. Each wheel/car gets its own exposure angle from abs(physical rad/s)*shutter seconds, bounded to a full circle. A symmetric64-sample arc integrates linear texture colour; rotated gradients preserve mip selection. Mirrored sidewalls need no opposite shutter orientation because the kernel is symmetric. Geometry, physical rotation, steering, normals and road/body pixels remain unchanged. No existing asset is replaced or upscaled.

Initial comparison uses8.333ms (approximately1/120s) fixed exposure. This is an authored presentation choice, not recovery of original GRID's shutter. A fixed shutter intentionally gives the same angular filtering at the same physical speed across30..300FPS. A conventional frame-relative shutter instead changes exposure with FPS; do not silently substitute one for the other. Low angular speeds approach the original texture continuously; stopped/paused/failed state uses zero angle. At large angles the bounded finite-sample kernel is approximate, not an exact analytic shutter integral.

2d4cc65 forward GPU1080p pilot and matched native4K8.333ms/off captures pass with eight sidewalls for player+one opponent; no shader/GPU warnings, identical final1200tick pose/handling. Images inspected: tyre lettering spreads along its circumference while spoke geometry stays sharp. This proves the sidewall effect runs and is confined to the selected material, not that the complete wheel is smooth in motion. Additional explicit30/60/144/300 virtual frame-clock runs are underway. These clocks test render/simulation scheduling; they do not measure achievable display FPS.

## Spoke geometry, next implementation

Rotating the rim atlas is invalid and cannot filter silhouette/holes between real3D spokes. Do not apply the sidewall shader to Wheel or Discs. Next evaluate a wheel-local radial scene-colour reconstruction using a snug swept hull plus depth/membership masking, per-wheel true angular velocity and fixed exposure. Preserve real rim geometry and restrict samples to the wheel; avoid smearing body, ground and nearby cars. Investigate front/back occlusion, grazing views, stationary brake calipers seen between spokes, and disocclusion before default promotion. A screen-space approximation must explicitly document missing information; a wheel texture/impostor swap is not automatically equivalent to3D shutter integration.

The native Bevy motion-vector path also needs prior spin and prior deformation vertices. Long linear vectors between two wheel poses cannot represent multiple turns or the curved trajectory of a spoke. A future full motion-blur pass must account for rotational motion rather than treating TAA as wheel blur.
