- New tool `tools/frame_spikes.py`: classifies the slow frame_stats windows of a set of runs. Render-gated means
  the worst render-thread frame (`rworst`) is within 1.5 ms of `max_ms`; otherwise the window is main-gated. It
  prints the dominant render stage of the worst frame, the main-thread split of the slow main frames, and the
  per-lap counts of frames over 3 ms.
