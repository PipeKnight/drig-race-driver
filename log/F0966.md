- The perf_test label of each run shows `mobile:mirror_every2` or `mobile:mirror_every3`. The `cockpit_mirror ... every=1` line still prints 1 because it logs the settings value at spawn; `place_mirror` reads the word every frame.
- The bench VERDICT agrees on head-cam GPU ms: every2 29.2 and 31.1, every3 25.0 and 25.0, off 18.9–19.9, every frame 27.0 and 27.6. The other cameras stay within about 1 ms.
- every2 costs more than drawing the mirror every frame: GPU 27.8 vs 26.8, render 38.1 vs 36.5, and frames ≥50 ms 18.7 vs 3.0 %.
- every3 saves about 2.6 of the about 7.5 ms that the mirror costs, but frames ≥50 ms rise to 7.3 %.
- Interpretation [I]: `place_mirror` toggles `Camera::is_active`. Each reactivation then costs more than a steady frame, probably because the mirror view is re-extracted and re-prepared. An interval would need the camera to stay active while its passes are skipped on the off frames.

Mirror cull and far plane (fallback, order lean, cull100, far200, twice) [O]:
- `mirror_cull100` cuts visible objects from 3100 to 2560, but d_opaque stays at 115, and head-cam GPU moves only from 26.8 to 26.3 ms.
- `mirror_far200` changes neither vis nor draws (26.4 ms).
- Whole runs are within 0.4 ms GPU of each other.
- Interpretation [I]: most of the mirror's cost is per-view work, not its geometry. The mirror camera copies the viewer's Hdr and Tonemapping, and Bevy draws directional shadow cascades per view. This is not measured; a per-pass GPU timing of the mirror view would show which part dominates.

- Uncertainty: two runs per arm. The every-frame row comes from the p20 fallback runs, not from the p18/p19 ABAB pairs. No screen capture was taken for stutter; the frame-time shares are the only evidence.
