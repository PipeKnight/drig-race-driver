F375 Render-height cap for the phone (`[video] render_height`, Android default 1080) and render/screen sizes in the FPS counter (2026-10-18, Claude Code). The owner asked for a cap: above Full HD makes no sense on a phone. Details: docs/ANDROID.md "Frame rate"; docs/SETTINGS.md `[video]`.
- `render_scale.rs`: `[video] render_height = native | 240..4320`; default 1080 rows on Android, `native` on the desktop. When the window is taller than the cap, the ViewerCamera renders into an `Rgba8UnormSrgb` image of the window's aspect ratio (3200 × 1440 → 2400 × 1080) and a `Camera2d` (order 1, MSAA off) shows it full-window through a UI `ImageNode`; at or below the cap it renders straight to the window, so never above native. Recreated on window resize. Windowed runs only; `--render` captures are unchanged. Pure size and parse logic unit-tested.
  - **HUD, pause menu, touch zones and FPS text are scaled with the scene** (UI on the 3D camera, drawn into the image; the ViewerCamera is also `IsDefaultUiCamera`). Keeping them native would need every HUD module retargeted to the window camera; not done. Their on-screen size is kept: the touch-mode UI scale is now min(h/720, image/window height), and without touch mode the scale is image/window height.
- FPS counter: second line `render of screen` (e.g. `711x400 of 1600x900`); `frame_stats` ends with `render=WxH screen=WxH`; a `render_scale ... mode=...` line per change.
