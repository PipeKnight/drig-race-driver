F798 Career team livery on the race cars (2026-11-10, Claude Code).
- Task (coordinator): backlog row 60's open career item "the team livery is not shown on the cars". Static analysis only (master Ghidra project via `grid_re.py decompile`, shader disassembly kept private); grid.exe was not run.
- Findings (docs/TEAM_LIVERY.md):
  - [V] 0x63FF70, 0x649C60 and 0x643460 build the team livery (0x5F6250) only for a car whose `vehicle_livery.file_string` ends in `_user` (0xCE67EC `_`, 0xCC321C `user`); 47 such rows, 44 car folders ship a `livery_user` package.
  - [V] 0x5F6250 holds the pattern (`livery_pattern.livery_image_name`, 0x5E9060 → 0x4F2440), the number (0x5E9160 → 0x4F23E0), three colours packed `0xFFBBGGRR` (0x5F1D50) and sponsors (0x649AB0, 0x642630).
  - [V] It draws only the `Car Body Paint` render instances on level-0 body-part joints: the scene visitor 0x6C4130 (vtable 0xCEE170) matches the joint id against `x0_` and 27 part suffixes (0xDB5308) and the shader instance name against 0xE4FD58[0], all with `strstr` (0xA66880). [O] The first port drew every triangle of the map; wheels, tyres, suspension and discs (second ST fixed at (0, 1), first ST in their own textures) then painted 99 % of the lac body texture in the primary colour.
  - [V] The compositor 0x6C7D40 (bind 0x6C53C0, release 0x6CC080) draws each triangle of `<car>_livery.pssg` in body-texture space (first ST) and samples the pattern at the second ST: `tinted = min(P.r·c0 + P.g·c1 + P.b·c2, 1)`, `out = lerp(tinted, base.rgb, base.a)`; `fill_gaps` bleeds drawn texels into their eight neighbours. [I] one fill pass, raw 8-bit arithmetic.
- Port:
  - `grid_formats::livery_map` reads the livery map (render sources with SkinIndices and two ST streams, no positions) and keeps the index ranges of the accepted instances.
  - `grid-app` `team_livery` composites on the CPU, builds the mip chain with the race-number helper (`car_numbers::opaque_dds`) and replaces `<car>_main.tga` of the player and the `teammate` car when their model has a `livery_user` package. Opponents of the player's model keep their shipped textures (their banks are filled before the player's are replaced). Uncompressed BGRA upload works in every graphics preset, so it is not gated on enhanced graphics.
  - Race profile key `[game] team_livery = <pattern file>,RRGGBB,RRGGBB,RRGGBB`; the career writes it for own-team events once `Career.team_livery` is set (driver offers race in the team's livery). `LiveryChoices` gains the pattern files and colour lookups.
  - Not ported: sponsor and number passes, the designer's 3D preview and colour grid.
