- Why: the shipped bonnet of both cars has a non-zero shake, which the port lacked:
  - damping 0.1798, min speed 20.8641 m/s, max 90;
  - scaleX 0.0001, scaleY 0.0024, rotationSpeed 0.0022.
- Traced [D] and emulated [E] by a research subagent:
  - 0x815960 is the last call of the Mounted update 0x822510.
  - The amplitude jumps to the ramp of the signed car speed (m/s) between min and max, then decays at shakeDamping per second, and stops below 1e-4. The shake time accumulates.
  - The eye moves along the view's right by n(0, 1) · scaleX and along its up by n(0.1, 1) · scaleY. A non-zero shakeAngle pitches the view by angle · n(0, rotationSpeed).
  - n reads a 6000-entry table (0xE7B230) at 300 entries per second, wrapping every 20 s. The static initializer 0x7F2B40 fills the table with a sum of four sines/cosines. **No RNG.**
  - The view's reset and activation zero the shake time and amplitude.
- [V] F470 run C, bonnet record:
  - +0x94 = 0.1798 in every row;
  - the amplitude follows the model;
  - above 40 m/s the vertical eye residual drops from 0.351 to 0.051 mm rms once the modelled shake is subtracted (correlation +0.989).
  - The bonnet's 4.7 mm rms forward residual is not shake (open).
