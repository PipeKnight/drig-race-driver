Owner test (Fanatec; needs the wheel base switched on before the game starts):
1. In `local\settings.ini` add, if not already there:
   ```
   [input]
   device = auto
   wheel_trace = on
   ```
   Start `Play GRID Race.cmd`. In the log (`local\playtest\manual-*.log`) look for `ffb_device product="..."` (the Fanatec should be listed with type 0x16 = driving), `drive_input ... wheel="<name>" preset=generic ffb=true axes=...` and `drive_force_feedback device="<name>" kind=wheel force=0.5 friction=0.5 effects=0.5`. If the line says `kind=rumble`, the wheel was not found or has no force feedback: send the `ffb_device` lines.
2. Pedals and buttons: with `wheel_trace = on`, press each pedal and the paddles; the log prints `drive_input_wheel_trace` lines with the axes and buttons. If throttle/brake are not Y/Rz or are reversed, set `wheel_accelerate`, `wheel_brake`, `wheel_pedals = normal`, `wheel_gear_up`, `wheel_gear_down` accordingly (FORCE_FEEDBACK §7.1), then remove `wheel_trace`.
3. Forces to feel (all at the defaults 5/5/5):
   - Standing on the grid: a heavy, even resistance (friction 25 scaled by 0.8, capped at 10 = full), no pull to either side; the wheel is not centred by the driver's autocentre (the game turns it off).
   - Driving: the friction fades between 2.5 and 6 m/s (9–22 km/h) to a lighter level; a self-aligning pull towards the straight-ahead that follows the front tyres' lateral force — stronger in fast corners, going light when the front slides (understeer).
   - Kerbs and rumble strips, grass and gravel: vibration that grows with speed above the surface's threshold.
   - Gear changes: a short kick on each up/down shift (none for neutral).
   - Hitting a wall or car: a jolt whose size grows with the impact; landing after a jump or a hard bump: a thump (suspension).
   - A damaged wheel: a thump once per wheel revolution; skidding: a vibration while the fronts slide.
   - Pause menu: the wheel goes slack; back in the race the forces return. Quitting leaves the wheel slack.
4. Settings: `feedback_force = 0..10` scales the self-aligning force, `feedback_friction` the resistance, `feedback_effects` the vibrations and kicks; `force_feedback = off` makes the wheel completely slack (input still works). Try 0 and 10 for each once.
5. Report: anything reversed (the pull pushing away from centre = sign error), oscillation on straights, forces that stay on in menus, or a crash; with the log file.
