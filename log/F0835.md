F835 Fence through smoke: scaled 3D image shown opaque (2026-11-16, Claude Code).
- Owner bug (F834, backlog row 128). Mac M4/Metal, Detroit derby route_8, chase_close, dynamic render 1498x936 of 2560x1600 (`local/owner-reports/fence-through-smoke/detroit-derby-mac.webp`): the chain-link fence behind the car is drawn dark and crisp over dense tyre smoke that hides the concrete wall below it.
- Root cause, verified on Windows:
  - With a render height below native (`render_scale.rs`; `Auto` is the Mac and phone default, and any `[video] render_height` below the window), the 3D camera renders into an `Rgba8UnormSrgb` image. A window camera showed it as a UI `ImageNode`, alpha-blended over the window `ClearColor` (0.14, 0.11, 0.06), which is 8-bit (36, 28, 15).
  - The source-coverage objects (`#object_simple_mesh.fx` two-sided objects, `foundation_backing.rs` `source_blend`) keep the saved separate-alpha ZERO/ZERO state (G283, docs/STATIC_OBJECT_MATERIALS.md). They leave alpha 0 in the image, so their pixels showed the clear colour. The smoke composite (`smoke_reduced.wgsl`, dst = src + T·dst) keeps the destination alpha, so the posts stayed dark through the smoke.
  - The Mac frame's post pixels (about 23, 25, 19) match a dark, warm colour seen through smoke. It is not Metal-specific.
- Ruled out by tests:
  - Smoke order and the smoke boundary: offscreen `--smoke-boundary off` hazes the fence like the default.
  - Reduced-smoke depth: offscreen runs at 1000–1500 rows haze the fence correctly.
  - Offscreen `--render` and native windows: the 3D camera draws straight to an RGB target, so they never showed it.
- Fix, general for every material and track:
  - `render_scale::SceneDisplay` is a UI material that shows the scaled image opaque (`scene_display.wgsl`: rgb, alpha 1; pipeline blend replaced), instead of the alpha-blended `ImageNode`.
  - The source alpha state stays as saved; any pass that leaves alpha below 1 no longer reaches the window.
  - Scope: 15 of 18 track `objects.pssg` reference `object_simple_mesh` (string scan), so most tracks were affected in the scaled mode.
  - The race view's own clear colour is opaque, so no intended transparency is lost. The menu scenes with `Color::NONE` clears (OSD, results art, livery carousel) are separate UI materials and are unchanged.
