- Change: `car_batch::patch`.
  - A dented group rewrites only the stale members of its built batches, instead of all members. A member is
    stale after a `Modified` mesh, or when it joins or leaves the batch; a member that left is written
    collapsed.
  - It writes only the bytes that differ, merges the bounds from per-member boxes, and streams only the
    changed runs through `mesh_stream`.
  - Any change of shape (layout, vertex count, members, batches) takes the full rebuild.
  - `static_batch::place` is now the vertex placement shared by `build` and `patch`.
  - Parts, damage and detachment are untouched.
  - `GRID_BATCH_PATCH=0` forces full rebuilds. The `car_batch` line has `patched=`.
