- Ground cover (`crates/grid-app/src/ground_detail.rs`, new `ground_cover.wgsl`):
  - The billboards are faced and faded in the vertex stage (vertex, prepass and deferred), using the CPU path's formulas and the main camera, which arrives through a material uniform.
  - Vertices hold the centre, the corner offsets in UV1 and the draw distance in colour alpha.
  - The CPU gathers instances within their distance plus 6 m in the old bucket order. The next gather starts on the async compute pool after 3 m and is streamed in place when ready.
  - Blended (Enhanced soft alpha) cover gathers again at once in a new bucket and keeps the exact per-frame sort centre.
  - Material: `ExtendedMaterial<StandardMaterial, CoverFacing>`.
- Skid marks (`skid_marks.rs`):
  - The mesh holds every strip slot at full length, and changes are written in place (mesh_stream) instead of replacing the asset each frame.
  - Unit tests: the triangles with area, their order, the packed bytes and the sort centre equal those of the old mesh.
- Unchanged: tyre smoke (already streamed, F392/F394) and source foliage (opt-in, off by default).
