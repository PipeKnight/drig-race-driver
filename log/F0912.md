- GPU [O]:
  - The opaque segment between frame_split markers is 27-29 ms.
  - Bevy's in-pass timestamps give `main_opaque_pass_3d` only 2.5-4.4 ms, with 1.5-1.9 M fragments.
  - Shadow cascade 2 reports 2.8 M primitives per frame, against 212 k triangles in `view_triangles`.

- Verdict [I]:
  - The phone is not pixel-bound; lowering the resolution further gains nothing.
  - The render thread and a resolution-insensitive GPU segment (likely tiler binning/geometry or the indirect-draw path, unverified) together gate the frame at about 35-37 ms.
  - The main thread (26-34 ms) is next in line.
