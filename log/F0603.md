F603 Phones without BC: OSD textures made before the upload path was chosen are converted; desktop check with BC off the device (2026-10-27, Claude Code, android-profile-apk; Owner 2026-10-27: the game does not start on a friend's Exynos phone (Mali-G77); must run on any GPU).
- Phone log [V]: Galaxy Note20 SM-N985F, Mali-G77 (Exynos 990), Android 13, APK 3c7a3516e27a bundled drift-nurburgring; copy in this worktree's `local/android/f-nobc/log-20261027-115418.txt`. `texture upload: gpu_bc=false gpu_etc2=true path=etc2-cache (no BC support)` at 20.515 s; from 22.453 s 48 × `Device::create_texture` "Texture format Bc3RgbaUnormSrgb can't be used due to missing features" (TEXTURE_COMPRESSION_BC), each followed by `write_texture` on the invalid texture; the `OsdMaterial` bind groups fail; `DeviceLost` at 24.6 s; `renderer exited with an error`. GPU preprocessing and clustering fall back to the CPU and work.
- Cause [V]: the upload path needs the device, which Bevy creates asynchronously and hands over only in the RenderPlugin's `finish` (bevy_render 0.19.1 lib.rs), so `texture_upload::configure` is a PreStartup system. `osd_scene::install` builds its 53 images while the app is assembled (`osd_scene` at 18.383 s, before the path line), through `texture_upload::image` but with the path still at its BC default: 48 of them are BC3 (63 images exist before PreStartup, 48 of them BC). The other loaders that call `texture_upload::image` do it in Startup or later (tyre_smoke, sky, track_surface, …). `main.rs` `graphics_texture_state` (the other Bc3 match) only counts BC assets; it creates nothing.
- Fix: `texture_upload::convert_existing` (PreStartup, chained after `configure`) puts every BC1/BC3 image already in `Assets<Image>` on the chosen path (ETC2 cache keyed by the BC levels, else RGBA8 decode); `convert_added` (Last, after the PostUpdate asset events, before extraction) does the same for any BC image a loader adds later, so no image path can bypass the check. Lines `texture upload: images_before_startup=N bc=M`, `texture upload: late_converted=N failed=F when=built|added`, and `late_converted` in the summary. The upload and the per-image conversion share `convert`.
- Desktop proof: a forced `[diagnostics] texture_fallback` (`etc2`, `rgba8`, `on`) now also leaves `TEXTURE_COMPRESSION_BC` off the device (`texture_upload::render_creation`, used by the windowed and offscreen RenderPlugin), so a desktop GPU rejects any BC texture as the Mali does. docs/SETTINGS.md and docs/ANDROID.md "GPUs without BC (F603)".
