F726 Pipeline warm-up at the race start: no objects missing while their pipeline compiles (2026-11-03, Claude Code).
- Coordinator task (owner, prototype 170678e6ce36): "иногда кадр словно моргает, я не понял это из-за обновления объектов или из-за чего" at the start of the Washington race; not captured by the owner. Backlog row 113; details in docs/PIPELINE_WARMUP.md.
- Reproduction: offscreen captures compiled pipelines synchronously, so they could not show it. New research switches: `GRID_CAPTURE_ASYNC_PIPELINES=1` keeps background compilation in a capture, as in a window; `GRID_PIPELINE_TRACE=1` (`_ALL=1` for every define) prints each new pipeline with its label and key defines, how many frames it waited, and each frame with any waiting.
- Cause [observed]: Bevy skips every draw whose pipeline is still compiling (`SetItemPipeline` → `Skip`). Pipelines are made on first use, so these hid their objects for the compile time:
  - the fades' `VISIBILITY_RANGE_DITHER` variants (F703, F708). Both levels of a fading object carry the range, so the whole object went missing;
  - masked prepass and shadow-cascade variants, materials first seen mid-race, tyre smoke, the F713 shadow proxy.
  - Washington race start, autopilot, 960x540, exe d5f99f5fe73d, 60 s (`local/blink/t60.log`): 19 pipelines created after the start, at render frames 271–2966. Two opaque dither variants made at frame 870, for kind 216 changing level at 27 m (about 680 px), waited 16 and 23 frames, so that object was missing for 0.3–0.4 s. The others waited 1–4 frames on this PC; the wait depends on the machine and its load.
- Not the cause, or not reproduced:
  - No repeated whole-frame blink in ticks 1–900, checked with `local/blink/blinks.py` (frames that differ from both neighbours while the neighbours agree) on `w-1`, `w-300`, `w-600`, `x-840`, `y-280` and `y-820`. At the start the scene is absent until all start pipelines and assets are ready (capture frames 0–35, then all at once).
  - Render-scale target changes: the owner's settings use `render_height = native`; `Auto` is only the Android/macOS default.
  - GPU occlusion culling is not enabled on any camera.
  - Not checked: the interactive window's presentation (`auto_no_vsync`).
  - No capture has a clear vanish frame: in `y-280` a car's level fade (slot 3, 75 m, frame 378) used an alpha-to-coverage dither variant that waited 2 frames, but at 960x540 behind a nearer car no missing frame is visible. So there is no before/blink/after trio; the trace is the evidence.
- Fix: `crates/grid-app/src/pipeline_warmup.rs` (shared path: every track, desktop and Android), once per process. A race runs in its own process, from the menu or on Android.
  - From the first frame with a viewer camera and a mesh, a black full-screen UI node covers the view.
  - Plain phase: every `Mesh3d` gets `NoFrustumCulling` (the cascades honour it too), every hidden non-UI mesh and parent is shown, and `ornament_lod::select` draws every level as a caster.
  - Once the cache has had no new or waiting pipeline for 8 frames: Dither phase. Every mesh also gets the step-0 fade range (drawn whole, dither variant), and every shadow-level part a step-0 shadow proxy.
  - Once quiet again: everything is undone. Ranges a fade replaced are kept. Each shown entity gets the visibility its own system last set, recorded through change detection.
  - Once quiet a third time, the cover lifts (at the latest after 900 frames).
  - The main world reads the render world's cache through a shared counter. `GRID_PIPELINE_WARMUP=0` skips the warm-up.
- After (exe f4174d5ce37d, the same 60 s run, `t60-fix2.log`): the warm-up ends at frame 59 with 93 pipelines. After it, 2 new pipelines in 60 s, both shadow-cascade prepass variants of a masked leaf material (diffuse transmission, second UV set) at frames 397–398, ready after 1 frame each. They do not appear with `GRID_ORNAMENT_FADE=0` (`t8-nofade.log`); which tree part needs them is not identified. Tyre smoke compiles mid-race when no smoke entity existed during the warm-up (1 frame).
  - Start capture `z-1` (ticks 1–300): black until frame 59 (before: the clear colour until 35), then the scene; no blink flagged. The cover makes the black start about 0.4 s longer here.
- Not covered: car levels hidden by `car_batch::hidden_layer()` (demoted meshes); meshes spawned after the warm-up.
