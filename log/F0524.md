F524 Build gate: launcher smoke, memory growth, tick-cost budget and a nightly long soak of the real app (2026-10-20, Claude Code, build-gate; coordinator's request: catch before each playable, portable and APK rebuild what the unit tests miss). Usage and design: docs/TOOLCHAIN.md "Build gate (F524)"; `grid-soak perf` in docs/SOAK_TESTING.md; `GRID_MENU_AUTO` in docs/EVENT_MENU.md. Hand-over: side-session note build-gate-F524.md.
- `tools/build_gate.ps1` (parts 1–3, 18–24 min) and `tools/build_gate_nightly.ps1` (part 4, overnight), shared code in `tools/build_gate/gate_lib.ps1`. They run the built `grid-app.exe` with its own data dir per run (`[osd] fps`, front end off, autopilot, `results_continue`), `GRID_AUTOPILOT=1` and `--drive-ticks`; windows minimized, effects at zero, and the gate's own audio sessions muted in the Windows mixer. Other grid-app processes are never touched. Never runs grid.exe.
  1. perf: `grid-soak perf` (new, `grid_tools::soak_perf`): one full-field quick seed per track (13), 3000 ticks, `GRID_THREADS=1`, pinned to the P-core threads (`--affinity FFFF`), process CPU time per tick (`GetProcessTimes`) against `tools/build_gate/perf_budget.tsv` (x1.3; wall p99 x3), up to 2 retries keeping the best. A case whose scenario digest changed since the budget is `changed` (2x check, `gate_perf_rerecord`), not a failure.
  2. memory: `tools/build_gate/memory-race.ini` (Donington, 12 Lacettis, 12 laps), 10 min on the wall clock, least-squares slope of the private bytes after a 2-min warm-up, limit 1.0 MB/s.
  3. smoke: every `settings/*.ini` plus `menu-default` (`grid-app --menu` with the new `GRID_MENU_AUTO` hook pressing Continue after 3 s and passing `--drive-ticks`; diagnostics only, no effect without the variable, not on Android), 60 s of driving each, 4 jobs. Fails on ERROR, "driving paused", decoder_failed/bank_failed/audio WARN, panic, early exit, timeout, peak private bytes over 4096 MB, FPS under 10, sim under 56 ticks/s.
  4. nightly: every private repro (`local/repro/*/career-race.ini`), every profile at full length (Le Mans 24 Hours' 720 s first) over seeds until `-Hours`, optional `grid-soak long`.
