F864 Contact dents: fixed lattice probes per part (2026-11-19, Claude Code).
- Coordinator task: the contact-dent cost in drive_present (`dpn`/`dpo`, 3-4 ms in single frames, left unchanged by F861): the same deformed meshes without a main-thread spike, general for all cars, vertex data and images identical. Details and tables: docs/PERFORMANCE.md "Contact dents (F864)".
- Finding: a field update moves 334 of the car's 360 lattice nodes (median = p90 = max over 24 updates, Lacetti against the barriers), so re-projecting only vertices near moved nodes saves nothing. A first version that did this measured 2.83 ms per update against 2.22 ms whole and was dropped.
- Change: `deformation_grid::Sampler::locate` / `sample_at` split `sample` (same arithmetic; `sample` = `sample_at(locate)`); `body_deformation::Field::probes` / `fits` / `displacements_at` keep, per part, where each point lies in the layout and its rest-lattice sample (fixed per car) and sample only the current lattice; `body_damage::Dents` (one per `DeformingPart`) keeps the bind-space points and triangles, sums normals as Bevy 0.19.1's `compute_smooth_normals` does (same terms, same order) without the mesh copy, and returns no mesh when the drawn one already shows the projection. Unsupported meshes (not an indexed triangle list, precomputed bounds) and refused layouts take the whole projection. Player and opponent dents use it on the compute pool; `GRID_DENT_FAST=0` keeps the old serial path for A/B.
- Exactness: unit tests `probed_displacements_have_the_bits_of_sampled_ones` (grid-tools) and `dents_have_the_bits_of_a_whole_projection` (grid-app; degenerate triangles, resets, inactive and refused fields) and the ignored real-car test `deformation_dents_have_the_bits_of_whole_projections` (81 parts x 24 updates): positions, normals, indices, bounds and the moved flag equal `project` bit for bit. Serial cost per update there: 2.29 ms mean (max 2.79) whole, 1.15 (max 2.07) new. Log local/dent/check.log.
