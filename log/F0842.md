- Goal (coordinator, item 5 of the F837 plan; owner target "100+ FPS under any conditions up to 2560x1440", the worst frames): remove the prop-break spike of up to 10 ms on the main thread; results identical. Details and tables: docs/PERFORMANCE.md "Main thread: prop breaks copy switches, not boxes (F842)".
- Root cause (temporary `PROBE` counters in `PropWorld::disable`, `local/mt/slow/pr-beach`, `pr-derby`): `Props.boxes` is an `Arc<Vec<PropBox>>` shared copy-on-write by the 12 cars' tracks and grid-app's flashback checkpoints. A break switched its boxes off with `Arc::make_mut`, so the first break after a checkpoint copied the whole box list (template `String`, eight positions `Vec`, shape) once per car: 12 x 3,621 boxes = 8.1 ms at Long Beach, 12 x 1,664 = 3.3 ms in the Detroit derby; later breaks before the next checkpoint changed the lists in place (1 us). The headless grid-opponent-race keeps unshared lists, so it never showed the copy. F839's "shared disabled-box list" experiment changed the loop, not the copy, hence no gain.
- Fix (`crates/grid-tools/src/entity_geometry.rs`, `dynamic_props.rs`): the collision switch moves out of `PropBox` into `Props.enabled: Arc<Vec<bool>>`; `Props::new` (every box on except the damage states' static ones) and `Props::disable` (intact boxes off, damage boxes on); the queries read `enabled[index]`; `PropWorld::arm` and `disable` write the switch list, the box list stays shared. A break now copies 3,621 bytes per car. Probe bins `grid-barrier-impact`, `grid-barrier-sweep`, `grid-line-near` read `enabled[i]`. Unit test `disable_copies_the_switches_and_shares_the_boxes`.
