F742 Ghost of the old frame through smoke after a resize: smoke materials now rebind the resized depth (2026-11-05, Claude Code).
- Coordinator task, owner retest of release 09da41d6 (PC). After a mid-race resolution change, a ghost of the car and of the old screen-size rectangle stays on screen, visible through smoke. Screenshot: `C:\Dev\Reassembly\local\owner-reports\resize-ghost\ghost.webp`. The top-left ~1214 × 758 holds a frozen barrier and car silhouette, and the smoke is cut along it. Backlog row 121.
- Cause [V code, Bevy source]:
  - The reduced smoke buffer (F645) samples a reduced scene depth image in the smoke material (`SmokeLight::depth`, binding 103).
  - On a resize, `smoke_reduced::size_depth` replaced the image and called `let _ = materials.get_mut(..)` to re-prepare the materials. Bevy 0.19's `AssetMut` emits `AssetEvent::Modified` only on a mutable dereference (`DerefMut` sets `changed`), so nothing was emitted.
  - The materials kept binding the old, smaller texture. The soft fade then read stale depth inside the old rectangle and nothing beyond it: the ghost.
  - Same class as F737 (an image replaced under a handle, a material that binds it). These are the only two images the app replaces or resizes in place; the render-scale image is re-created and re-assigned.
- Fix: `resize_depth` replaces the image and dereferences each smoke material once, then logs `smoke_depth size=WxH rebound=N`.
- Checks:
  - Test `a_depth_resize_marks_the_smoke_materials_changed`: one `Modified` per material, none for an unchanged size. Targeted `cargo test --release -p grid-app -- smoke_reduced osd_scene`: 6 passed, 1 ignored.
  - `tools/hud_window_check.py` (silent autopilot window, interpolation on, mid-race resize 1280 × 800 → 2560 × 1369) now also requires a `smoke_depth` line at the new size with materials rebound.
    - On the fixed exe: `PASS: OSD bound to the resized texture at 2544x1330; smoke depth 1272x665 rebound by 5 materials`.
    - The exe of 09da41d6 has no such line, so the check fails it.
  - Shots before and after: `local/ghost/smoke2-{before,after}/`, crop `local/ghost/ghost-before-after.png`. [N] Those frames have too little smoke over the old rectangle's edge to show the ghost, so the crop is not evidence. The evidence is the mechanism, the test and the window check.
