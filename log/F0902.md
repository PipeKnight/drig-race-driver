F902 Row 137: public builds never contain DLC or other original files (2026-11-24, Claude Code).
- Owner request: after allowing all sources (F901), the owner asked to move the DLC cars and tracks and the cut content to PC and include them in DRIG builds, so that players need not get GRID and the DLC separately, since the DLC never shipped on PC.
- Coordinator answer: declined bundling. The DLC, prototype and base game files belong to EA/Codemasters; a public release containing them redistributes protected content and risks a takedown of the whole repository; players would still need their own GRID for the base game. Offered alternatives (1)-(5), now in row 137: enable cut content from the player's PC install, load DLC from the player's own Mac/console copy, ship only project- or community-authored openly licensed content, private PRIVATE-marked builds for the owner's own devices, or permission from the rights holder.
- Owner agreed and asked to record it in row 137. Studying DLC, mods and the prototype (F901) is unchanged.
