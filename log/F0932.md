F932 Dynamic resolution: a GPU-bound frame time in the hold band counts as late (Mac countdown) (2026-11-26, Claude Code).
- Fixes F929 item 2. On the owner's Mac (build 9406cb12), the countdown rendered at the 1440-row ceiling at 55-59 FPS, with 12.5-13 ms of swap-chain acquire against 3 ms of main thread. The smoothed frame time of 17.2-17.9 ms stayed inside the hold band between `ON_TIME_MS` 17.2 and `LATE_MS` 18, so the first step down came only after GO, or at "1" in fullscreen.
- Change, the same on every platform:
  - `crates/grid-app/src/frame_split.rs`: `last_acquire_ms()` returns the render world's last acquire, stored at the End point on every frame, with the counter shown or not.
  - `crates/grid-app/src/render_scale.rs`: `Dynamic::update` takes that wait.
  - A frame is GPU-late when it ran over `TARGET_MS` but would have been on time without the wait (`frame_ms - wait_ms <= TARGET_MS`). Its time over the target is smoothed into `gpu_late_ms`.
  - Inside the band, the frame counts as late when `gpu_late_ms >= GPU_LATE_SHARE (0.5) * (smoothed frame_ms - TARGET_MS)`.
  - Everything after that is unchanged: 20 late frames, the warm-up, the probe, the hold and the 15 s block.
  - `render_dynamic` change lines print `gpu_late_ms=`, and the install line prints `gpu_late_share=`.
- Why this form [I]: seeding the race height from the warm-up was rejected because the warm-up frames are compile hitches of 197-211 ms. With the acquire rule, the following frames keep today's behaviour:
  - frames late for the CPU (a long main frame that hardly waited);
  - on-time frames with a long vsync wait, which have no time over the target;
  - 165 Hz desktop frames of about 6 ms, which never reach the band;
  - phone frames of 25-40 ms, already above `LATE_MS`;
  - a backend where a GPU-bound frame waits somewhere other than acquire: `gpu_late_ms` stays low there.
- Test `render_scale::tests::dynamic_height_steps_down_when_the_gpu_holds_frames_in_the_band`. It models the Mac countdown: above 1392 rows every eighth frame takes 25 ms, the rest take `TARGET_MS`, with 4.5 ms of CPU and the remainder waiting.
  - The test requires a step down to 1392 rows or fewer within 1.5 s after the warm-up, no "ineffective" change, under 12 s of the minute spent in the band above 1392 rows, and at least 1296 rows after 60 s. A hand trace puts the step at about 3.35 s.
  - The same frames late for the CPU change nothing, and `gpu_late_ms` stays under 0.05. 3600 on-time 60 Hz frames change nothing either.
  - `cargo test --release -p grid-app --bin grid-app -- render_scale:: frame_split::`: 18 passed, 0 failed (`local/ab/f931-test.log`).
