F991 Phone display-mode tests: MIUI ignores preferredDisplayModeId; cap 120 neutral (2026-11-27, Claude Code).
- Task (coordinator): the display-mode tests left over from queue 3 (120 Hz with cap 120, 40 Hz with cap 40, 30 Hz with cap 30) before the owner's APK; same rules as F987 (start ≤38 °C, abort above 45 °C, our package only). The switch is `-SetEnv GRID_DISPLAY_MODE_HZ=<n>` (F948): `MainActivity.preferDisplayMode` sets our own window's `preferredDisplayModeId` to the supported mode of the current panel size nearest n. The plan said: if the mode change is ignored too, record that and stop; no MIUI workarounds beyond our own window attributes.
- Setup: Adreno 740 phone, APK 2fde6a80 (F983 single-threaded schedules, F967 no mirror), `-PerfTest lean`, render_height 480, no presses. All runs started at 37.9–38.0 °C and ended at 38.0–39.6 °C.
- Probes [O] (60 s each, `local/android-bench/cpu1/d0-probe/`):
  - The requests succeeded. Each returned a mode of the 1440x3200 panel: 120 Hz = id 2, 40 Hz = id 4, 30 Hz = id 5. The supported rates are 60, 120, 90, 40 and 30.
  - The display did not follow. `android_display_hz` read 60 Hz at start-up in all three runs and never logged a change. The same holds for the `setFrameRate` hint (`result=0`, `display_hz=60`), as in F928.
  - The mode the window was on just before the request varied between ids 2 and 1. So MIUI does switch the panel for other windows; it keeps ours at 60 Hz.
  - The 30 Hz and 40 Hz modes are therefore ignored too. With caps of 30 and 40 the app holds 31/30 and 40/38 FPS (median/p10). Both are below the 43–44 FPS at the default cap, so neither is a default candidate, and no pairs were run for them.
  - The 120 Hz probe gave 47/43 FPS over its 60 s, which is why the cap pair below was run.
- Cap pair [O] (`local/android-bench/cpu1/d1-cap120/`): ABAB plus one extra arm, 200 s / 30000 ticks, race seconds 20+. F928 had said to repeat cap 120 once the GPU frame fell under about 22 ms; it is 16.6 ms now.

- Verdict: no display mode and no cap becomes a default. The Android cap stays 60 (row 153 updated). On this phone (MIUI) the app cannot get 120, 40 or 30 Hz through its own window attributes, so per the plan there are no further attempts. On a phone that honours the request, 40 FPS on 120 Hz (3 vsyncs) is still the open idea of row 153.
