F747 Row 122: tree shadows turned with the camera; sun-facing shadow twins (2026-11-05, Claude Code).
- Owner confirmation on release b6eaecae, relayed by the coordinator: the earlier problems seem fixed, namely the ghost after a resize (F742, row 121) and the house blink (F743/F745, row 115). Both rows are now closed.
- New owner report on b6eaecae: "тени от деревьев сильно меняются в зависимости от ракурса камеры, под определенным ракурсом их вообще не видно, так не должно быть".
  - Evidence: `C:\Dev\Reassembly\local\owner-reports\tree-shadow-angle\5.webp`–`8.webp`, a stopped scene on Washington with the orbit camera around the parked car.
  - From behind the car and from above, the road shows dense dappled tree shadows; from the side and the front it shows almost none.
- Cause [V code; Bevy source for the layer filter]:
  - Facing foliage (`trees/facing` leaf quads, `trees/sheet` distant sheets, `trees/card` impostors) is expanded on the CPU towards the main camera each frame (`source_trees::present`).
  - The sun's shadow pass draws that same mesh. From views where the quads stand edge-on to the sun, the leaves cast almost nothing, so the shadow turned with the camera.
  - The original's facing vertex shaders face each pass's own view (SOURCE_VEGETATION.md), so its shadow pass faces the sun. This was the open "per-shadow-view facing" item there.
- Fix (shared path: every facing placement on every track, cards and sheets included):
  - Each placement gets a sun-facing shadow twin (`source_trees::ShadowTwin`). The twin is the same centres expanded along the sun's right/up; sheets and cards are expanded about world Y towards the sun, and cards use the atlas view seen from the sun.
  - The twin is rewritten only when the sun turns. It has the same material and bounds as the part and sits on `car_batch::shadow_layer()`.
  - `swap_shadow_twins` runs after Bevy's light visibility check and before `shadow_cull`. In every sun cascade list it replaces a part by its twin and drops a twin Bevy took by itself, so the twin casts exactly when and where the part would: level, fade and cull.
  - Ornament D-crossing proxies of facing parts use the twin's mesh.
  - First attempt [V, failed]: twins on the hidden layer 30 cast nothing. Bevy 0.19's `queue_shadows` drops a mesh whose render layers the light does not see.
- Test `shadow_twins_face_the_sun_whatever_the_orbit_camera_does` checks four things:
  - the twin's corners lie in the plane across the light;
  - the twin's mesh is unchanged while a `ViewerCamera` orbits the tree at four yaws (the orbit and replay camera mode);
  - the twin replaces its part in a cascade list and is marked visible;
  - a twin without its part in a list is dropped.
  - Targeted `cargo test --release -p grid-app -- source_trees ornament_lod shadow_cull`: passed (source_trees 20 after the last change).
