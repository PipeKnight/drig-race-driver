Groups `object_simple_emissive`, `object_lod_emissive`, `object_animated_emissive` and `object_simple_night_emissive*` bind `TEmissiveMap`, which the port never read. Signs, shop fronts, screens and night windows showed as their dark diffuse.
- Fix (shared path, every route and object):
  - `object_materials::emissive_materials` reads each such material's emissive binding by its full `container#name` key and its `EmissivePower` / `EmissiveColour`.
  - `configure_emissive` adds it as a Bevy emissive texture: simple/lod `E`, animated `E·colour·power`, night `E·EmissiveTime·power/3`. The light unit is the route's sun lux over the `backlight_suncolour` peak over π (`graphics::light_unit`).
  - `EmissiveTime` follows the original 0x9790E0 (`night_emissive_time_init`, symbols.tsv): the day/night `emissive` curve for timeline routes (Le Mans 24 h), 3 for forced-night routes, 0 otherwise. `update_night_emissive` follows the Le Mans clock.
  - `GRID_OBJECT_EMISSIVE=off` leaves the maps out, for matched comparisons.
  - Not ported: `object_emissive_ripple` (flagged `dark_unlit_emissive`), and the night shader's shadowmask light term.
