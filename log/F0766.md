F766 Crowd gasps and the AI engine Treble low-pass (rows 70, 72) (2026-11-09, Claude Code).
- Row 70, ported: the crowd gasps (CrowdRecoil), from static RE only; the original was not run.
  - Every car's contacts feed the visual crowd's recoil records (0x756E40 → 0x756C40). Contacts within 1 m merge; at most ten records per frame. The flag means the other body is static geometry and the contact is not floor-like.
  - On the next frame (0x608940 → 0x76A620), a record whose summed impact exceeds 2000 broadcasts `CrowdRecoil`, with a 1 s cooldown (0x7648F0).
  - 0x41BCD0 starts the gasp. 0x449FA0 then starts at most one `group_gasp`/`grand_gasp` per tick on query entry j, in slot j. The level is gaspControl(intensity) + the cell's curve(people) + gaspDistControl(distance), with −36 dB as the floor. Pitch is 0.95–1.05 over the distance.
  - Code: `grid_tools::crowd_audio::{Gasps, Recoils, Recoil, Crowd::recoil, gasp_step}`, `collision_audio::read_varieties` (shared), and grid-app `crowd_sound`, which feeds the contact impacts. Docs: AUDIO_RESEARCH §34 (§31 updated).
- Row 72, static comparison: AUDIO_RESEARCH §35.
  - 41DE10 gives the player's car Treble 0 in every driving view, so the player's engine has no low-pass and a filter is not why it sounds "artificial".
  - AI cars get lin2db((1 − min(0.02 d, 1))^8) − 3 dB, from −3 dB at the car to −51 dB at 25 m.
  - A25D90 and A27780 turn this into AL_LOWPASS_GAINHF = 10^(Treble/40).
  - Ported: `audio_mix::{ai_engine_treble_db, treble_gain_hf, Lowpass}` and `engine_audio_continuous::Mixer::treble`, set per opponent each frame in grid-app `engine_sound`. The player's mixer stays at GAINHF 1, an exact pass-through, so the player's engine output is unchanged.
  - The resampler and mix period of the original (wrap_oal 2.2.0.5, Generic Software) are inside the third-party DLL and were not read, so the linear-resampler and 16 ms questions stay open.
- AUTHORED decisions [I]:
  - The EFX low-pass design is a 5 kHz high shelf with slope 1 (the Audio EQ Cookbook form OpenAL Soft uses).
  - The mechanical voices (whine, backfire) are not filtered.
  - DetachedMultiCar gives Treble 0, because its reference point (car +0xA460) is unknown.
  - The +0xA6B4 fade and the voice filter-preset offsets (0xEBEE28) are not modelled.
  - The gasp sample choice uses the port's crowd RNG.
