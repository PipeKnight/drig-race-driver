F584 Car part batching (2026-10-26, Claude Code, focused-torvalds; coordinator relaying the owner, 26 Oct: phone rendering round 2, step 1: merge each car's static, undamaged parts per material and split a part out when damage or detachment needs it; the desktop picture checked by fixed-frame image comparison; report entity counts and timings, the phone estimate marked [I]).
- New common module `crates/grid-app/src/car_batch.rs` (`GRID_CAR_BATCH=0` turns it off):
  - each car's non-wheel, opaque or masked parts merge into one mesh per material in the car's frame, baked by `static_batch::build` (now `pub(crate)`);
  - batched parts keep their entities and components and move to render layer 30, which nothing draws;
  - each frame a part stays in its batch while it is at its rest place, visible, and keeps its material and mesh. Otherwise it leaves: loose or detached panels, smashed glass, cracked-glass materials. It rejoins after 30 frames back in place;
  - a deformed member mesh rebuilds its batch the same frame.
- `asset_counts` reports the kind `car_batch`. `span_profile`: tables list 60 spans and 30 schedules, and `GRID_PROFILE_TOP` overrides the span count for local runs.
- Counts [O], Donington phone view, frame 3,600, drawn: opponent parts 961 → 375 plus 108 batches; player parts 107 → 33. `car_batch` line: 888 parts, 108 groups, 867 batched at the end, 1,132 rebuilds (827 for deformation), 83 ms of rebuilds in all.
- Picture [O]: not bit-identical.
  - Renders: 1600 × 900, `--render-fixed-step`, Donington, on against off with the same executable.
  - Changed pixels: tick 300 0.085 % (largest channel difference 20), tick 2,400 0.21 % (29), tick 6,000 0.17 % (24).
  - The changes are single MSAA samples on coplanar stickers, where the baked positions round differently. The same holds with shadows off. Crops look the same, and repeated renders of one path are identical.
  - Cause: body and sticker surfaces lie coplanar and z-fight. The batch bakes each part's rest matrix into its vertices, and these round differently from the per-part model matrices.
