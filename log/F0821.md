F821 Loading stat panel and particle emitter evidence (2026-11-11, Claude Code).
- Task: coordinator item 3 after F810: build the loading screen's `loading_stats` panel and its particles, reusing hud's `fe3d.rs`/`fe3d.wgsl` unchanged. Static analysis only; grid.exe was not run. Details are in docs/FRONT_END_FLOW.md §2 ("Which fact is shown", the segment table, "The cycle", "Particle emitter").
- Findings, stat panel [D static]:
  - Each of the 38 `fact_*` slots carries a mode mask (0x4EA770). The collector 0x4EF1A0 builds the current mask with 0x4D6D40 and never sets 0x20 (online) or 0x40 (awards). The 38-case builder 0x4E4ED0 fills the three texts and decides whether a slot is valid. The valid slots are shuffled with MSVC `random_shuffle` (0x4F4510), and 0x4DFAD0 steps through them.
  - The cycle runs in 0x52B970 off the flags at +0x20C (busy/refresh/shown) and the on/off timers 0x515180/0x5152A0. Play-in 0x504A30 starts `loading_%d_in`, play-out 0x504AC0 starts `loading_%d_out` with the same N = `range(1, definedanimations+1)`. Each event seeks `Anim!1` to its segment and plays 1.333 s of it over 1.3 s (in) or 0.8 s (out).
  - The values come from a profile statistics object (getters 0x6A1850…0x6A1B10). The port has no such object.
- Findings, particles [D static]: `pfx=testEmitter` is a plain NODE. The `pfx` prefix makes it a `GlyphParticleSystem` (node-type table 0x4B09B0, factory 0x4BFB10, vtable 0xCBCB28). It overrides only clone, message and dirty marking, and has no emission. No file or exe string names an effect for it. `pfx_fe_data_set.xml`'s three FE systems are referenced nowhere, and `pfx_frontend_data_set.xml` lists ten files that do not exist. [I] Nothing connects an effect to the loading world, so the port draws no particles there.
- Port: `crates/grid-app/src/loading_stats.rs`, installed by `loading_screen::app` when the world loads (`GRID_LOADING_STATS=off` leaves it out for A/B shots).
  - It reads `screen/loading_stats=loading_stats` from LOADING.package. The glyph is expanded through `osd_glyph::expand_with` with the saved locals. Each text line follows its helper chain through `Anim!1`.
  - It uses `persistentdatacommon.xml`'s timing block, the `loading` lighting group and reflection cube (`spec.reflection`), the line's fonts from `frontend/fonts`, and the dirt and pattern maps. Drawing goes through `fe3d::plugin`/`fe3d::mesh` and `osd_scene::{cube, font, text_mesh}` without forking them.
  - Facts: career results always shows. Owned cars and driver offers show when the career save exists (`career::save::load`). The other slots have no statistic in the port and stay invalid.
  - The order uses the MSVC shuffle seeded from the clock. Timing follows the desired on/off times (no load estimate).
  - Startup prints `loading_stats lines=.. animated=.. facts=.. mask=..`; each new fact prints `loading_stats fact=..`.
