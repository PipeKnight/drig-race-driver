F703 Ornament level and draw-distance fades (owner pop report) (2026-11-02, Claude Code).
- Owner report (2026-11-02, prototype dc4408b43366, through the coordinator), verbatim: "объекты вдалеке иногда внезапно появляются на экране, вместо того чтобы плавно появляться сначала в низком качестве, а потом более детально. Вообще в идеале ничего прямо на твоих глазах появляться не должно". In English: distant objects sometimes suddenly appear on screen instead of first showing at low detail and then refining; ideally nothing should appear right in front of you. FIDELITY_BACKLOG row 111.
- [O] The original has no fade or hysteresis: it switches ornament levels and culls at D (`lod_distance_02`) within one frame. The one exception is scenery types, which cross-fade levels over a 20 m distance band (`xFadeWeight`, TRACK_LOD 3.1). The port did the same (F633).
- Measured: new research switch `GRID_ORNAMENT_LOD_TRACE=1` prints one `ornament_change` line per change with distance, D, levels, scenery flag, screen size and NDC. On a Washington autopilot drive (6,000 frames, `local/shadow/layout/pop-trace-wash.log`) it counted 2,452 changes. 1,126 of them were appearances at D (1,087 with the box centre on screen), among them scenery buildings appearing at 750–1050 m as boxes 100–330 px across. Ruled out:
  - the camera far plane (Bevy's default 1000 m): Bevy 0.19's CPU culling (`intersects_obb(.., true, false)`) and GPU culling (`mesh_preprocess.wgsl`, 5 planes) both skip the far plane;
  - ground detail: it already fades in its shader;
  - trees (`source_trees.rs`): a static closest level with no culling.
- Port [deviation, owner request]: every level change and every D crossing fades over 0.4 s (`ornament_lod.rs` `Fade`, `fade_range`, `FADE_SECONDS`):
  - The old and the new level are drawn with Bevy's complementary 4×4 dither (`VisibilityRange` ramps 10^6 m wide, so the step does not depend on the distance; 33 ranges).
  - Going back to the level that is fading out reverses the fade. A camera move over 20 m in one frame switches at once.
  - The shadow level fades with the instance's presence.
  - The A2C coverage shaders (`foundation_backing.wgsl`, `object_coverage_aux.wgsl`, Codex's) apply the same dither, and `foundation_backing.rs` admits `VISIBILITY_RANGE_DITHER`. Without that, the first fade panicked ("unsupported auxiliary/alpha variant").
  - `GRID_ORNAMENT_FADE=0` keeps the hard switch.
- Cost of the first version, which gave every ornament a range (executable e4f018ec9b56; Washington 1920x1080, `--benchmark-frames 3600`, one pair, other sessions' builds running):
  - frame mean 21.7 → 30.6 ms;
  - MainSchedule 14.8 → 25.1 ms;
  - main opaque GPU pass 3.11 → 3.68 ms.
  Bevy checks every ranged entity each frame and re-extracts all of them when any range changes, and the dither variant discards, so the pass loses its early depth test. The range now sits on an entity only while it fades. A first build of that (c72ea5c414cd) lost whole hoardings and fences once they had faded: Bevy 0.19 re-specializes a pipeline only on a `Mesh3d` or material change (`check_entities_needing_specialization`), so the dither variant outlived the removed range and discarded every fragment. `select` now marks `Mesh3d` changed on each insert and removal. The pushed source differs from a9d073de25c9 only in taking `Mesh3d` and `Time` as optional, so the selector's lifecycle test (a part without a mesh, an app without `Time`) still runs. 
- Cost of the final version (a9d073de25c9, fade off vs on, the same executable, interleaved; PC loaded by other sessions' gates throughout):
  - GPU, fixed-step motion runs from tick 4800 (15 five-second windows each; per-window primitive counts match, so the scenes are identical): main opaque pass 2.05 → 1.95 ms. No GPU cost.
  - CPU, `--benchmark-frames 3600` pairs: the quietest pair gave frame mean 18.6 → 17.6 ms and MainSchedule 11.7 → 9.7 ms. The other pairs disagree in both directions. In the fixed-step pair, DriveTick (pure simulation, untouched by the fade) went 5.7 → 10.2 ms along with MainSchedule 20.3 → 39.1 ms, which is outside load.
  - [I] Few entities carry a range at a time: about 0.4 instance changes per frame lasting 24 frames, so on the order of ten instances fade at once, against thousands of entities before. The remaining CPU cost is below what this PC can resolve under load.
