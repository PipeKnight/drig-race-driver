F818 Car dust, scratch and damage gates and GhostCarValues (2026-11-11, Claude Code).
- Follow-up of F815. Static analysis only; no grid.exe runs. Write-up: docs/CAR_REFLECTIONS.md 1.8 (the original) and 4.2 (the port); docs/CAR_SHADER_MASKS.md.
- Original [O, static]:
  - The three paint vertex programs that declare `dirtLevel` (basic, part state, wobble; private `local/shaders/vp_dirt/`) all write o7 = (1 − vertex red, 1 − vertex green, sat(dirtLevel), 0).
  - In the paint pixel program, the reflection weight is ×= sat(1 − 2·m1.r·dirt)·(1 − scratch.a·y)·sat(1.1 − x). The dust gate also scales the highlight and the metallic factor. Shadow = max(`GhostCarValues.w`, mask).
  - In the window glass program, the alpha rises with the reflection weight and the highlight, both scaled by `GhostCarValues.z`. Thin panes fade by sat(4.5a).
  - `GhostCarValues` is set to (1, 1, 1, 0) by 0xA14540. The car scene update 0xA18460 rewrites it only while the render record's fade (+0x60) changes; who writes that fade is not traced.
  - Mud and dust set-up:
    - every track's `mud.xml` gives the dust colour #3B3834, read by 0x9ABE70;
    - 0xA10B30 sets `MudColour`/`DustColour` per vehicle;
    - 0xA10710 (vtable 0xD161E8) and 0xA108D0 collect the paint parameter indices, using the name table 0xE4FC98;
    - surfaces carry `dirtbuildup`/`mudbuildup` rates;
    - 0x63D0B0 reads `max_maintained_dirt/scratches/dents`.
  - The per-frame dirt and scratch writers are not traced.
- Port (shared path for every car, livery and LOD):
  - `car_materials::shader_controls` stores (1 − r, 1 − g) of each saved vertex colour as the car mesh's UV_1 (LOD0 and the low LODs). The batching key already carries UV_1.
  - `car_reflection_overlay.wgsl` adds:
    - the dust gate (`DIRT_LEVEL` = 0, untraced accumulation);
    - the scratch gate (AUTHORED: the scratch map is unbound and counts as alpha 1; no saved paint vertex has green < 255);
    - the damage gate, which cuts red-free vertices to 0.1;
    - the shadow floor from `GHOST.w`;
    - the window glass alpha path with `GHOST` = (1, 1, 1, 0). Binding 11 holds `ghost`; `sun_program.z` holds the dirt level.
  - On glass, keep = clamp((1 − α)/(1 − a), 0, 1). This replaces F815's AUTHORED "weight × pane alpha" for tuned window glass. Lamp glass and untuned glass keep it.
  - Not ported: the `DustColour` albedo tint (0 at level 0), dirt accumulation, scratch map binding, phone path (overlay off).
