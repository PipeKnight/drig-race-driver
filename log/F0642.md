F642 Dynamic resolution: high-DPI 1440-row ceiling, frame-time steps, inconclusive probes (2026-10-29, Claude Code).
- Owner request after F640: improve how dynamic resolution works on the Mac. The MacBook log (Apple M4, 3024x1890, Detroit Derby, F640) showed three things: rendering at the native 1890 rows; 48-row probes lost in noise; a −46 ms probe sending the height back to native during the worst smoke.
- crates/grid-app/src/render_scale.rs:
  - `auto_range(rows, scale_factor)`: on a window with a scale factor above 1, the desktop Auto ceiling is at most `HIDPI_CEILING_ROWS` = 1440 and the floor is half of that. On the MacBook that is 2304x1440 upscaled, 58 % of the pixels. `RenderScale::scale_factor` is read from the primary window in `apply`.
  - `RenderHeight::platform_default`: `auto` on macOS too, since a Mac's built-in display is high-DPI. Other desktops keep `native` (owner, 2026-10-26). A Windows display scaled above 100 % gets the 1440 cap only under Auto.
  - `Dynamic::step_down`: the next height is where a pixel-bound frame would take 16.7 ms, rows × √(16.7/frame_ms), in whole 24-row units. The step is at least `DOWN_ROWS` (48) and at most `MAX_DOWN_SHARE`, a quarter of the height. Phone, 720 rows at 45 ms: 720 → 552 → 480 instead of five 48-row steps. Mac, 1440 rows at 68 ms: 1440 → 1080 → 816 → 720.
  - Probes:
    - a probe whose frame got slower by more than the expected gain is inconclusive: the lower height stays and later steps continue;
    - a step that gains less than half the expected gain without slowing (CPU bound, F571) still returns to the ceiling for 60 s.
  - `HITCH_MS` raised from 100 to 250 ms: dense smoke held 87–102 ms frames for seconds.
  - Log lines are now `render_dynamic version=3`. `event=install` adds the step share, the HiDPI ceiling and the hitch limit; `event=range` adds `scale_factor`.
- Tests:
  - new: `dynamic_height_hidpi_range`, `dynamic_height_steps_by_the_frame_time`, `dynamic_height_keeps_a_step_when_the_scene_gets_slower`;
  - the existing step tests now expect the larger steps: 552 instead of 672/624, and the Video-screen ceiling case uses 504;
  - `cargo test --release -p grid-app --bin grid-app render_scale`: 11 passed.
