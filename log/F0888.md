F888 Build lock hold limit and step timeouts (2026-11-23, Claude Code).
- Why: a prepush full gate held the machine-wide build lock for more than 172 min because a grid-app GPU test hung, and every session queued behind it. A leftover `grid_app-<hash>.exe` also kept a timing script's open-ended wait busy. Owner approved the task on 2026-11-23.
- `tools/build_lock.ps1`: every hold has a limit (`-HoldMinutes` on the wrapper and on `Enter-BuildLock`; 0 or less is refused, so there is no unlimited hold).
  - At acquisition the holder joins a named Windows job object (C# P/Invoke, `Local\grid-build-lock-<pid>-<ticks>`), so orphaned test binaries stay members.
  - A detached watchdog (`-Watchdog`, started before the holder joins, so it is not a member) waits until the deadline plus `-GraceSeconds` (120). If the same pid, pid start and acquisition time still hold the lock, it kills the job members, the holder's descendants (tracked every 5 s) and the processes started after acquisition from the checkout's target/build dir. It then appends a line to `timeouts.log` in the lock root, which frees the lock.
  - The wrapper runs its command through `Invoke-BuildLockProcess`. At the deadline it kills the tree, prints `build_lock: TIMEOUT … exit 124` and exits 124.
  - `Exit-BuildLock` stops the watchdog, kills leftover test binaries of the job and appends each hold to `holds.log`.
  - `-Status` shows the time held and the limit, and flags `OVER LIMIT` with the watchdog's state.
  - The outside-the-lock report now also lists grid-app.exe, grid.exe, cargo test binaries (`name-<16 hex>.exe`) and apitrace.
  - `GRID_BUILD_LOCK_ROOT` moves the root, for tests only.
- `tools/prepush.ps1`: per-step deadlines and a gate hold limit (`-HoldMinutes`, default crate 65 min, workspace/full 55 min). A step at its deadline:
  - fails as TIMEOUT with exit 124;
  - the verdict names the step and quotes libtest's "has been running for over 60 seconds" lines and the last 15 log lines, also in `summary.md` and `timeouts.log`;
  - the lock is released.

- New `tools/build_lock_test.ps1` (private temp lock root, never the real one; kills only its own tagged processes and watchdogs). It runs five cases:
  - wrapper with an orphaned grandchild at `-HoldMinutes 0.2`;
  - a normal command;
  - a nested holder;
  - a holder that ignores its limit (`-Status` OVER LIMIT, then the watchdog);
  - a prepush-like step timeout through a harness.

  Command: `powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\build_lock_test.ps1 -Root <scratch dir>`. Verdict: `build_lock_test: PASS 5/5: wrapper-timeout ok (14 s), normal ok (2 s), nested ok, watchdog ok (19 s for a 3 s limit + 15 s grace), step-timeout ok (11 s, killed 4)`.
- Bugs found by the test:

  - A test pipe held open by a surviving orphan blocked `Out-String`; the test now redirects to files with a 120 s wait.
