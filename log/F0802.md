F802 Car reflections: the original's material tuning and SpecularValues floors (2026-11-10, Claude Code).

- Backlog row 100.
- Finding [O, static only; the original was not run]. The original does not draw cars with the Fresnel and occlusion controls saved in their pssg. 0x99C300 (`car_materials_xml_load`) reads `raceload.jpk#materials.xml` into 35 nine-field records, one per name in the table at 0xE4FD58. Each record starts from the fallback [300, 64, 10, 4, 0.2, 1, 2, 0.1, 0.9].
- Every frame, 0x9E7360 (called at the end of the vehicle manager update, 0xA10310) runs while the reload flag +0x558 is set. Both the loader and the constructor 0xA135C0 set that flag.
- For each material of the 20 cars, 0x9E7360 takes the first table name that the material's name starts with, case-insensitive. It submits:
  - `SpecularValues` = (highlight power, strength, 0.25, 0.5);
  - `MetallicSpecularValues`;
  - `FresnelValues` = (power, min, max, 0).
- The floors 0.25 and 0.5 are the vehicle manager's +4 and +0, set by its constructor. Earlier docs took them as 0.
- Effect: car paint goes from a flat Fresnel weight of 0.45 (pssg power 1, 0.3/0.6) to 0.27 facing and 0.86 grazing (2.13/0.27/0.86, occlusion 1.05). Cabin, grills, suspension and tyre wall have min = max = 0 and do not reflect. `Grills` and `Matt` shadow `Grills Glass` and `matt clipping shader`.
- Details: docs/CAR_REFLECTIONS.md 1.6 and 4.2. Symbols: 0x99C300, 0xB9DD10 `material_set_vector_param`, 0xA10310 `vehicle_manager_update`, 0xE4FD58 (already named `livery_material_names` by F798); 0x9E7360 is now [O].
- Port: `car_reflection_overlay::Tuning` reads the installation's `raceload.jpk` once in `load_package` (log line `car_reflections tuning=raceload.jpk#materials.xml`). `Controls::from_constants` takes the tuned record:
  - Fresnel power, min and max;
  - on paint, the occlusion scale;
  - the floors `FLOORS` = (0.25, 0.5), passed to the paint program's z/w through `Controls.floors`.

  Untuned materials keep their pssg controls with floors of 0.
- Not ported:
  - the records' specular and metallic fields, which feed the lighting (Codex's desktop look; a follow-up);
  - the floors on glass, because the port's glass has no occlusion or shadow gate.

  - tuned controls and floors;
  - the prefix and order rule;
  - the installed file: paint 1.05/2.13/0.27/0.86. Every car's high LOD has 461 reflecting materials, all tuned (paint program 166). 42 of them now reflect nothing.
