F761 Ambient fill at the original's weight: compensate Bevy's 0.45 diffuse ambient response (row 125) (2026-11-09, Claude Code).
- Found by the route graphics audit (F760): shadowed and ambient-lit ground was much darker than the F716 fill intends on every route (Milan route_3 road strip under a building shadow near black).
- Root cause: Bevy lights a diffuse surface's ambient with `EnvBRDFApprox(albedo, F_AB(1, N.V))`; without the `dfg_lut` feature `F_AB` at roughness 1 is 0.4524 for every N.V, so the ambient reached the surface at 45 % of the fill. The original weights `ambient` by 1: `albedo * (sun sat(N.L) shadow + back (1 - N.L)/2 + ambient) * ao` (docs/ROAD_MATERIAL_RESPONSE.md; ambient uniform 0x0074369C `lighting_uniform_ambient_colour`, docs/SOURCE_SKY_LIGHTING.md; symbols.tsv port column now names graphics::Fill).
- Fix (shared path, every route and preset): `graphics::BEVY_DIFFUSE_AMBIENT = 0.4524`; `Fill::from_lighting` and the authored-lighting fill (effective_lighting.rs) divide the ambient brightness by it. `GRID_BASELINE_FILL=linear-bevy` renders the fill before this change, for comparisons.
- Matched static renders (same exe, same camera, before = `linear-bevy`, after = default; `local/route-audit/fill/*-pair.png`): Milan route_3 mean luminance 81.6 -> 94.4, pixels with max channel < 20 0.328 -> 0.182; Washington route_0 (camera 214,2.4,874 -> 223,1.0,893) 104.7 -> 118.1. Sunlit surfaces unchanged; no clipping seen in the crops.
- Whole audit, chase shots of all non-night routes (366 shots, `local/route-audit/full` before vs `full2` after; autopilot positions match only roughly): mean luminance 119.6 -> 132.4, pixels with max channel < 20 0.006 -> 0.004; largest gains on Milan (route_3 shot 3 88.2 -> 103.0).
