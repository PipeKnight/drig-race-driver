- Cause [V] (profile build, 1 s windows): the worst frame was under the warm-up cover, in the window with `pipeline_warmup phase=Restore` and `water_probe capture=start`; the whole frame was Bevy's `prepare_view_upscaling_pipelines`. The water probe's six Rgba16Float faces are the first views with that format, and their blit waited behind the ~23 pipelines the Restore phase had just queued (82.6 ms/frame over 20 frames Enhanced cold, frame max 1705 ms).
- Change (`crates/grid-app`):
  - `blit_prewarm.rs` (new, render world `Queue` set): specializes the blit, without waiting, for every window surface format and every `RENDER_ATTACHMENT` image view format as soon as it exists (replace blend, plus alpha blend where the adapter can blend the format; default compositing space and every space a camera uses; depth and integer formats skipped). The face images exist at scene build, so their blit compiles during loading and the later block returns at once. Shared for all tracks, cars, modes and image cameras, because the keys come from the targets that exist. `GRID_BLIT_PREWARM=0` skips it.
  - `pipeline_warmup.rs`: without the block, the probe-view pipelines compiled in the background while frames rendered, about 5x slower (last one 56 frames, ~1 s, against 169 ms inside the blocked frame), so the cover lifted 0.3-1 s later. While the cover is up and the cache has waiting pipelines, the render thread now sleeps 20 ms after each frame (`compile_yield`, `Cleanup` set; `GRID_WARMUP_YIELD_MS`, 0 = off; 50 ms lifted no sooner). The phase lines print `ms=` since the warm-up began.
