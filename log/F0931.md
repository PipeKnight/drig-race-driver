F931 Steam Deck estimate from E-core and UHD 770 stand-ins (2026-11-26, Claude Code).
- Task (coordinator, owner's request): estimate DRIG's Steam Deck performance on this PC, with no Deck and no other device. Method, tables, model and caveats: docs/STEAM_DECK_ESTIMATE.md.
- Result [I]: at 1280 x 800 on the phone bench's race (Donington, 12 cars), the Deck should run
  - desktop defaults at 35-64 fps, GPU-bound (render thread too at the slow end);
  - the lean mobile preset (480 rows) at 41-66 fps, bound by the CPU.
  - Neither preset is assured of 60; both hold 30 on average. The E-core runs put one 50-80 ms frame in almost every second, so the lean preset's p99 estimate is 39-63 ms. Whether the Deck has those spikes is open.
- New `tools/deck_estimate.ps1` and `tools/deck_estimate.py`:
  - Up to nine configurations: P-cores or E-cores × RTX 4070 or UHD 770 × desktop defaults or lean preset, plus P-cores + UHD desktop at 480 rows for the GPU's fixed/per-pixel split.
  - The CPU topology is read with `GetSystemCpuSetInformation` (E = logical 16-19, mask F0000; P = 0-15, mask FFFF [O]). Each process is started with `start /affinity`. The adapter is chosen per process with `WGPU_BACKEND=vulkan` and `WGPU_ADAPTER_NAME`. No system setting was changed.
  - Each run gets its own data dir: the bundle's settings.ini with a 1280 x 800 window without vsync, the fps overlay on and sound at 0, plus a bench.ini with the phone bench knobs, `force_feedback = off`, autopilot and `camera_presses = 30,5`. Runs are muted.
  - All runs go under one build-lock hold. The script refuses the owner's prototype exe.
  - The analyser checks per run: adapter, Vulkan backend, affinity against the topology mask, coverage, unfocused windows (>10 % is a problem) and at least 4 camera presses. It prints the table, the present gap comparison, the Deck model and one VERDICT line. `-SelfTest` runs the analyser selftest and the topology read.
- New diagnostic `[diagnostics] camera_presses` / `GRID_CAMERA_PRESSES` (crates/grid-app input.rs, settings.rs; docs/SETTINGS.md).
  - It replays the phone bench's touch camera schedule on the desktop: every period, Change View (held 0.15 s) and Look Back (held for the look time) alternate.
  - Each press logs `camera_press`. Test `camera_presses_follow_the_phone_bench_schedule`.
- Measurements [O] (`local/deck-estimate/matrix1`, 30000 ticks, medians of 447-469 1 s windows):

  | config | fps | main | render | GPU | p99 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | E + RTX desktop | 60.7 | 10.9 | 14.5 | 5.2 | 81.1 |
  | E + RTX lean | 57.6 | 12.1 | 15.4 | 4.6 | 75.7 |
  | P + UHD desktop | 26.1 | 9.9 | 33.1 | 35.7 | 40.8 |
  | E + UHD desktop | 25.0 | 18.6 | 36.6 | 35.2 | 51.2 |
  | P + UHD lean | 55.8 | 7.9 | 16.9 | 14.9 | 20.0 |
  | E + UHD lean | 39.7 | 16.2 | 22.9 | 14.5 | 64.9 |

  - The UHD GPU is 10.3 ms fixed + 24.8 ms per megapixel.
  - With the UHD, the render thread's prepare costs 3-5 times and meshes about 2 times what they cost with the RTX on the same cores.
  - The cross-adapter copy (UHD frames shown on the RTX's monitor) has no consistent CPU-side cost: the desktop pair's gap differs by 1.7 ms, the lean pair's by -0.2 ms.
- Run problems [O]:
  - The 480 s deadline cut every run 11-41 s before the 30000th tick; 457-466 race s remain. The default deadline is now 600 s.
  - The first P + RTX desktop run's window was unfocused throughout and held 60.0 fps with `frame_cap=none`: a limit outside the game, likely the NVIDIA driver's background frame cap [I]. The rerun, focused, ran the whole race: 146.8 fps, main 3.7, render 5.9, GPU 2.2, p99 8.3 ms.
- Model [I]:
  - GPU: (fixed / compute + per-pixel / bandwidth) / driver. Compute 1.29-2.08, bandwidth 1.72-1.99 (Deck 88-102 GB/s against this PC's dual-channel DDR4-3200 at 51.2 GB/s [O]), driver 1.0-1.3.
  - CPU: E-core costs × 0.85 at best, at worst the larger of × 1.3 and the E + UHD costs without the GPU wait.
  - Frame = max of main, render and GPU, plus the measured gap. Ticks per frame are solved as a fixed point.
  - Caveats: no SMT on the stand-in, Intel driver against RADV, Windows against SteamOS/Proton, the 15 W shared TDP, one run per configuration.
