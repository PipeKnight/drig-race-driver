F758 Haruna's vertical streaks are light-shaft cards (2026-11-09, Claude Code).
- Follow-up to F757 (no code change). The tall beige vertical streaks in two Haruna close views (local/water/hw-after views 0 and 3) are not water.fx. They are the two `god_rays.fx` instances of Haruna's objects.pssg, drawn by the shared light-shaft port (docs/LIGHT_SHAFTS.md):
  - `01 - Default`, texture `godwater.tga`, DustSpeedY -1.5, GodStrength 1, GodDensity 0.5: a waterfall card;
  - `godfog_03`, texture `godfog3.tga`, GodStrength 0.3, GodDensity 1: fog.
- The port logs `light_shafts prepared materials=2 strength=1.0405 density=1 sun=(2.96, 2.09, 1.02)`. The program colours the cards with the sun's backlight colour, hence beige.
- Static only (grid-shader-constants and an objects.pssg node scan). Their strength against the original is not verified without a capture.
