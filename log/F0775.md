- Compaction (general, every car; `replay/frame.rs`, `replay/stream.rs`): each car's 78 per-frame words are predicted from its last three frames. A word is predicted linearly (`2a − b`) when that predicted the previous frame's word better than a repeat, else repeated. Floats are compared through an order-preserving integer map. Residuals are written zigzag, then varint, behind a 10-byte nonzero mask; a flag replaces a mask equal to the last one. A key frame comes at every chunk start and every 64 frames. Shared items (geometry, deformation, broken flags) stay referenced by frame number, and `forget_before` re-writes them. The decoder caches its history and is reset on a cut. Measured [V] on race.ini (12 cars, tick 900, 433 frames): 735466 bytes (about 1.7 kB a frame) at the first replay; 812515 bytes (about 1.9 kB) at the second replay after the jump-in cut.
