- Change (`crates/grid-app/src/skid_marks.rs`):
  - The strip slots are a `Pool`. It has no `DerefMut`; `IndexMut` marks the slot touched, so the compiler routes every mutation through the tracking. Assigning a checkpoint, a new pool and the test-only `iter_mut` mark every slot.
  - `changes()` compares only touched slots. Of a changed strip it reports the quads from the first to the last that differ (`changed_quads`, over `quad_inputs`: the quad's segment, its two points, and the last point for an unused quad), or all 32 when the bank or `linked` changed. Runs are global quad indices.
  - `pack_quads` packs a quad range, and `present` writes it at `run.start * 4` vertices.
  - Strip bounds use `quad_corners` (positions only) instead of the full vertices.
  - In test builds, `changes()` asserts that an untouched slot still equals its written copy (Debug text, so NaN payloads compare equal).
- Tests: `changed_strip_runs_rebuild_the_whole_buffer` now also covers a checkpoint restore, and a pool filled by 1,500 short strips (steals, retiring, fading). After every write it checks that the streamed bytes equal a whole repack and that the cached centre equals the full scan. Release skid tests: 88 passed (`local/mt/testskq.log`).
