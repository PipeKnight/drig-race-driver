F837 Mesh uploads per frame: crowd cards, car batches and part meshes stream in place (2026-11-17, Claude Code).
- Goal (coordinator, Phase 2 of F836; owner target 2026-11-17 "100+ FPS under any conditions, at any resolution up to 2K, on this PC"): remove the per-frame mesh re-uploads generally, with no image change; measure before/after on Windows, including the worst frames. Details and tables: docs/PERFORMANCE.md "Mesh uploads per frame (F837)".
- `GRID_MESH_UPLOADS=1` (`crates/grid-app/src/mesh_upload_probe.rs`, new): every 5 s `mesh_uploads`, the top changed meshes (`mesh_upload`) and owner groups (`mesh_upload_group`), plus bytes written in place per owner (`mesh_stream_group`, from a per-mesh tally in `mesh_stream.rs` that runs only with the probe).
- Sources found (about 5,000 uploads/s in both scenes) and general fixes:
  - `crowd_billboards::update_cards`: every cell in range replaced its positions and normals at 15 Hz or on 0.5 m eye moves. Now the same cards are packed straight into the mesh's GPU allocation (`mesh_stream::write`, `pack_cards`); a new asset only when the cards change.
  - `car_batch`: a dirty group rebuilt every batch mesh, also for levels not drawn. Now only while the level is shown, posed or fading (`drawn_soon`); a batch with the same usage, vertex count, attributes and indices gets only its changed vertex runs (`stream_changed`, `mesh_stream::write_at`; asset updated untracked). The log line gains `streamed=`.
  - `main.rs` `drive_present`: staged part meshes are skipped when `body_damage::same_shape` holds.
- Statistics: `frame_stats` gains `p99_ms`, `over10`, `over16` and a sparse frame-time histogram `ft_hist=` (last field); `tools/frame_split_smoke.ps1` prints the pooled 1 % low (bucket), slow-frame shares, `p99_max_ms`, the slowest window's split and idle state at start and end.
