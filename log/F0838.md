F838 Tree billboards faced on the GPU: a compute pass writes the mesh slabs (2026-11-17, Claude Code).
- Goal (coordinator, item 1 of the F837 plan; owner target 2026-11-17 "100+ FPS under any conditions, at any resolution up to 2K, on this PC"): stop facing every source-vegetation billboard on the CPU and re-writing its vertices each frame (90-230 MB/s, F392), with no image change. Details: docs/PERFORMANCE.md "Tree billboards faced on the GPU (F838)"; docs/SOURCE_VEGETATION.md.
- `crates/grid-app/src/source_trees/gpu_facing.rs` and `tree_facing.wgsl` (new): a compute pass in `RenderGraphSystems::Begin` writes the faced POSITION, NORMAL and card UV0/UV1 into the vertex slabs (made `STORAGE` through `MeshAllocatorSettings::extra_buffer_usages`). Placements are registered once on `Changed<Facing>`; per frame only the shown placements and the view are sent. Jobs are grouped per slab into aligned bindings within the storage binding limit. The shader mirrors the CPU math. `source_trees::present` writes on the CPU only where the GPU path does not take the mesh.
- Compute pass rather than the vertex shader: the facing materials are shared with shadow twins, ornament proxies and the card material and swapped by LOD and fade code; the pass leaves every material and the shadow path unchanged. Shadow twins are still faced to the sun on the CPU.
- Fallbacks to the old CPU path: `GRID_TREE_FACING=cpu`; no compute shaders or fewer than 4 storage buffers (install returns early); pipeline not compiled yet; meshes in slabs without `STORAGE` (large-object slabs).
- Tests: `windows_start_aligned_and_stay_within_the_binding_limit`, `placements_reuse_sources_and_slots_and_reset_with_the_track`; `cargo test --release -p grid-app --bin grid-app -- source_trees`: 23 passed, 1 ignored; Clippy clean.
