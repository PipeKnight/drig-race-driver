F738 Track surfaces take the route's macro AO scale; Washington black void gone (row 119) (2026-11-05, Claude (session fervent-napier); Coordinator relayed owner playtest of ee26c2ebc427: another black texture void at Washington after the JVC bridge, right side under a raised deck; find what is missing, compare with the original, fix in the shared path, sweep routes).
- Spot: Washington route 0, right of the track after the JVC bridge, under the raised plaza deck. Reproduced in a static render from the playtest pose (`menu-30285-32178.log`, car stopped at -277.9, 20.7, -4.8 at 65 s; camera -274.47,23.10,0.06 looking at -283.64,21.70,-13.01).
- What it is [O, port diagnostics]: not a missing mesh. `--geometry-ray` finds the tracksplit infield `infield1/2!4` (`track_infield.fx`) under the deck. Temporary shader flag renders (not committed) show `track_surface` draws those pixels with a non-black albedo and macro alpha below 0.2; its lit output is below 0.01, so after tone mapping the pixels are (1,1,1).
- Cause: the port applied the macro AO at scale 1 (`ao = macro.a`); with the sun shadowed under the deck, ambient times that AO went to black.
- Original [O, static RE]: `track_infield.cg.fp` computes `ao = 1 + ambientOcclusionScales.x (macro.a - 1)` (`mad r1.y, c49.x, ...`), and the lit colour is multiplied by `ao`. 0x743750 publishes `ambientOcclusionScales` from lighting object +0x160. 0x730F90 packs lane x from +0x298, which 0x72BB40 reads from lighting.xml `sao_scale`; its default is 0.9 (0x726A50). The scale is per route (0.58-1.0); Washington is 0.9.
- Fix (shared path, every track surface group, all tracks, presets, Android): `graphics.surface_ao_scale` from the route's `sao_scale` (default 0.9) replaces the fixed 1 in `track_surface` `controls.z`. `GRID_SURFACE_AO_SCALE=1` restores the old AO for review. This is a class fix: every track-surface pixel with low macro alpha brightens the same way on all routes; elsewhere the change is small (reference road 55.3 -> 56.6 sRGB).
- Evidence (`local/r53/`, one build, `ab.sh`): void region 4,677 pixels, mean sRGB 1.0 at scale 1 vs 31.6 with `sao_scale`, 0 black after. `void-pair.png`: before | after crop (dark paving under the deck instead of a black hole). The owner's screenshots are in the main checkout's `local/owner-reports/row118/`.
- Open: the original may still be darker or lighter there. It also multiplies the screen DAO mask, does not tone-map, and its shading space is unverified. A grid.exe capture at this spot would settle it (owner's go).
