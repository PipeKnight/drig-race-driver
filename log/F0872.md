F872 Owner request: HDR monitor output and settings (2026-11-19, Claude Code).
- Owner request (2026-11-19, future): support HDR monitors and their settings. Recorded as FIDELITY_BACKLOG row 141. The order is the same as row 140: it goes into the restored Options menu.
- Observed in the dependency sources (not yet tried in DRIG): wgpu-hal 29.0.4 Vulkan sets the swapchain colour space to `EXTENDED_SRGB_LINEAR_EXT` (scRGB) when the surface format is `Rgba16Float` (vulkan/swapchain/native.rs:168). No colour-space code was found in wgpu-hal's dx12 or metal folders. bevy_render 0.19.1 view/mod.rs has no output colour-space setting. So Vulkan on Windows, our default backend (F868), has a base. Picking the surface format in Bevy, Metal EDR and Android are unverified.
- The larger part is the display transform. Today tonemapping maps to SDR [0, 1]. HDR needs a transform that keeps the original grade up to paper white and extends only the highlights (sun, sky, bloom, lights, reflections). The HUD and menus must be drawn at paper white.
- Settings: HDR off / on (auto when the display reports it), peak brightness in nits, paper white (UI and mid-tone brightness), and a calibration screen.
- Checks: SDR output must stay identical when HDR is off. Test on a real HDR display (is one available to the owner?), and on the Mac M4 XDR screen for EDR. Measure the frame cost of the FP16 swapchain in the FPS cells.
