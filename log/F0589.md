F589 OSD timer box sounds are online-only; speech and music sized (2026-10-27, Claude Code, car-series; Coordinator 2026-10-27: OSD timer/countdown research with a size report, port if medium or smaller; then report speech and music sizes).
- Research [O], docs/AUDIO_RESEARCH.md §28. Static only; GRID was not launched. The start lights, drift cues and flashback were already ported (F452). What remained was the OSD timer box's `countdown_timer` / `countdown_go`.
  - The box is `object/messagebox=counter` (vtable 0xCEC0B8, update 0x6AD220 `osd_timer_update`, start 0x6A0390 `osd_timer_start`). On each change of the whole-second number it plays `countdown_timer`; at 0 it plays `countdown_go`.
  - The start is queued by 0x6B0790 `osd_queue_timer`, which asks the provider at OSD +0x214 (vtable +0x1C).
  - The single-player race-session base 0x7F0500 `race_session_get_timer` returns false.
  - The only override found, 0x6F9D70 `online_session_get_timer`, belongs to the online (Demonware) session: a timer of at most 30 s in session modes 4 or 0xB.
- Conclusion [I]: the timer box beeps are online-only, so there is nothing to port offline. The override search could miss a provider of a different shape; this is stated in §28.
- Side finding for the HUD owner: the port shows 3, 2, 1, GO in the messagebox at every race start (`race_osd.rs`). The original single-player session never starts that box.
- Sizes (§28), so the coordinator and owner can choose:
  - Music is large. The original does play race music: `music0%i.WIM` random 1–8 while enabled (`race_music_option`), `raceend.WIM` at the end, replay streams with a channel-pair offset (0x4127C0 `music_choose_stream`). The eleven WIM streams (330 MiB, 32 kHz, flags 0xF0) use a custom compressed codec (7.7 bits/byte, no Ogg or RIFF framing). That codec must be reverse-engineered before any port.
  - Speech is large. 2.5 GiB in 5 languages; English has 3814 lines, plain PCM16 mono, in 21 folders. The triggers live in eventSpeech.bin and audioEventSpeech.bin and the two speech managers. A first slice (start, position, end of race) is medium.
- Symbols: 0x69FF90, 0x6A0390, 0x6AD220, 0x6B0790, 0x7F0500, 0x6F9D70, 0x4127C0 named.
