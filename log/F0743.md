- Coordinator task, owner retest of release 09da41d6: F740 did not stop the blinking; many Washington houses blink at least once while driving towards them, especially in the first corners. Owner: "Пусть ищет соседние кадры, где отличается яркость. Это и есть моргание больших объектов на экране". Only 2560 × 1440-class window runs count as evidence.
- New research tools:
  - `[diagnostics] window_frames = START,SECONDS` (`GRID_WINDOW_FRAMES`, frame_pacing.rs) appends every frame the window shows, box-averaged by 8, to `<data dir>/window-frames.rgb` and prints `window_frame frame=N` with Bevy's `FrameCount`. Test `a_reduced_frame_has_the_header_and_box_averages`.
  - `tools/blink_detect.py`:
    - computes per-tile mean luminance (32 × 18) of consecutive frames;
    - flags 2-D regions (≥ 4 tiles) that jump against the frames before and after while the rest of the screen moves smoothly, by more than 4 times each tile's local median one-frame change (floor 4 of 255);
    - writes prev | during | next crops;
    - its verdict splits the events into scenery (above row 11) and the chase car's zone below.
  - `GRID_SHADOW_TRACE=1` (shadow_cull.rs) prints `shadow_dropout` for each caster that is in a sun cascade's final list in frames N-2 and N but not N-1.

- Cause [V code, Bevy source; I for the mapping to every owner sighting]:
  - A level change keeps the shadow level, but while the shadow level fades out as a colour level (1 → 0 when approaching), its part carries the fade's `VisibilityRange`.
  - Bevy 0.19's `check_dir_light_mesh_visibility` applies visibility ranges to light views as well. At the fade's last step (16 of 16) the range excludes every distance, so the part left every cascade until the fade ended and `select` removed the range: one frame at 0.4 s fades and 60 fps.
  - The shadow pass itself takes no dither (TRACK_LOD section 5), so the only visible effect was that one frame without the shadow.
  - Trees (shadow level 0, fading to the card) behave the same way.
- Fix (shared ornament path, every track, desktop and Android):
  - `shadow_proxies` also casts through a proxy at full presence (tag 0) while the shadow level fades as a colour level; the part is `NotShadowCaster` meanwhile, as for D crossings (F713).
  - Every proxy now spawns with the part's `GlobalTransform` and `Aabb`. Before, a new proxy kept the identity transform until the next propagation, so its first frame cast from the origin.
  - Test `the_shadow_level_casts_in_full_while_it_fades_as_a_colour_level`. Targeted `cargo test --release -p grid-app -- ornament_lod shadow_cull frame_pacing`: 15 passed.
