AUTHORED: support-point impulse contacts against the track only (friction 0.7, restitution 0.2 above 1 m/s), the track plane from three `ground_below` casts (slopes), the rule that a panel sleeps only lying on its broadest face (off it, it is turned over its support edge at up to 40 rad/s^2 / 3 rad/s, standing in for the thin shell the box hides), a 20 s give-up, and a final per-corner cast that lifts a resting panel out of any kerb or dip the plane misses. The launch (car velocity + outward push + spin) stays AUTHORED: without contacts against its own car a panel with the car's motion only would sink through the body.
