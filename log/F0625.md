F625 Cockpit: rear-view mirror rendered as the original (512x128 every frame), cost at the phone's resolution (2026-10-27, Claude Code, camera session; coordinator, owner's task: cockpit 1:1 — driver and hands, steering wheel, gauges, mirror).
- First step of the cockpit series: the mirror. Static analysis through the master Ghidra project; the original was not launched. Details, port table and known differences: docs/COCKPIT.md "Mirror". Private evidence: worktree camera-dynamics-11a55d `local/cam/cockpit/mirror/findings.txt` (with mirror.fx disassembly and UV dumps).
- Original [D]:
  - the pass "rear view mirror" (0x5F73F0) draws into a 512 × 128 target (hardware settings `<mirrors width height>`, no MSAA) every rendered frame;
  - one camera sits 0.5 m ahead of the mean centre of int_reflection_l/m/r and looks back along the car (fov 15° vertical, near 0.5, far 1000, cull 500);
  - it runs only while the interior is shown and a mirror is in the main frustum;
  - it draws the world, cars at their coarsest LOD within 200 m, characters, trees and ornaments, with no interior, particles, shadows or post-processing;
  - mirror.fx samples (1 − u, v) and blends to a black tint at 0.5, so the reflection shows at half brightness;
  - `<mirrors enabled>` is never read.
- Port (`cockpit.rs`):
  - `MirrorGlass` and `MirrorCamera`: an Image target with an unlit material at 0.5 and UVs flipped at load;
  - the cockpit's parts sit on render layer 28, which the driving camera and the sun take and the mirror camera does not;
  - `cull_mirror` sets the 500 m visibility frustum;
  - `no_mirror_shadows` gives the mirror's sun cascades reject-all frusta: lit, no shadow casters;
  - `GRID_MIRROR` (`off` or `WxH[/frames]`) for measurements;
  - the Performance test Auto cycle gains a step `off:mirror` (15 steps).
- Orientation [V, render]: the camera's image right is the car's left. After the flip each wing mirror shows its own side, and "KUMHO TYRES" and "ECSTA" read reversed in the left mirror.
- Image proof (fixed-step renders, `local/cam/render/render.ps1`, `compare.py`): chase_close, chase_far, bonnet and bumper are 0 pixels different from the F618 renders (`local/cam/render/after` vs `mirror2`). The head-cam differs only inside the mirrors.
- Cost at the phone's resolution (desktop RTX 4070, 2308 × 1080 window, mobile preset 1539 × 720, head-cam, 11 opponents behind, autopilot, uncapped; `local/cam/mirror_cost/ab3`, run under the build lock), mean frame time:

  | Mirror | Frame time | Cost |
  |---|---|---|
  | off | 6.11 ms | |
  | 512 × 128 every frame | 7.52 ms | +1.41 ms |
  | every second frame | 7.72 ms | +1.61 ms |
  | 256 × 64 | 7.49 ms | +1.38 ms |

  - Before the cull and shadow trims (`ab1`, noisy shared PC): about +3.7 ms, with 1.07 M mirror triangles over five views.
  - GPU pass time is unchanged at about 1.1 ms: the cost is the extra view's CPU work.
- Decision, by the owner's pre-approval ("Ну отражение в зеркале обычно можно отрисовывать или в более низком разрешении, с меньшим количеством FPS и так далее. Так что если оно будет дорого стоить, это решаемо"):
  - a lower resolution or rate does not lower the cost (a skipped frame costs more: the camera's view state is rebuilt when it turns back on [I]);
  - so the phone keeps the original's 512 × 128 every frame, before and after alike;
  - [I, not measured] on the phone, about 4–6 ms of a head-cam frame (CPU about 3–4× slower, F380); the `off:mirror` Auto-cycle step measures it there.
  - Phone-resolution frame: `local/cam/render/phone/head-cam.png` (private).
