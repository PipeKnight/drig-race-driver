- What happened in F556 [V, reading the code; no phone log of 94e06d136784 was available]:
  - The dynamic controller ran only while the setting was a row count. A saved count replaced the 720 ceiling, and the Video screen showed only that number, never the height rendered.
  - It treated a frame as CPU-bound when the whole main schedule (First..Last) took over 80 % of the frame or 14 ms. The F549 phone profile (APK 6087fbcafa30) measured the main app at 19 ms of a 25 ms driving frame and 10.7 ms of drive_tick per pre-race frame. [I] So late frames counted as CPU-bound and the height most likely never dropped. The owner is right in effect.
- Changes (crates/grid-app):
  - `render_scale::RenderHeight::Auto` (`[video] render_height = auto`): dynamic between `mobile::DYNAMIC_FLOOR_ROWS` 480 and `mobile::RENDER_ROWS` 720. It is the Android (mobile preset) default. A row count is now a fixed height, and `native` turns the scaling off. `GRID_DYNAMIC_RES=0` keeps Auto at 720.
  - Work measure: only the fixed-step loop (`RunFixedMainLoop`, Before/AfterFixedMainLoop), the simulation. Hold thresholds: 50 % of the frame or 16 ms (a whole 60 FPS frame; above the paused pre-race 10.7 ms).
  - While late frames are held as simulation-bound, `render_dynamic ... reason=cpu_bound` is logged every 10 s with rows unchanged. The next phone log will show why a height does not drop.
  - Migration (`settings::RENDER_HEIGHT_VERSION` = 2, `[video] render_height_version`): with the mobile preset, a saved render_height without the version reads as Auto once. The Video screen writes the version with every save, so a fixed height chosen now stays fixed. `Settings::video_render_height()`. The desktop reads saved heights as before.
  - Video screen Render height: Auto / 720 / 900 / 1080 / Native. Auto shows the rows rendered now, e.g. `Auto (624)` (`FrontEnd::auto_rows`, updated by `apply_video` from `RenderScale::dynamic`).
  - Performance test screen: the Auto cycle row and `perf_auto` are removed. Kept: the 7 switches (Shadows, Foliage, Ground cover, Smoke/debris/skid marks, HUD, Sample logging, Whole scene), `GRID_PERF_TEST=auto` and the other `GRID_PERF_TEST` switches on the desktop. Any switch on the screen ends a running cycle.
