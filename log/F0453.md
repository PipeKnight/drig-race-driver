F453 Audio parameters paced by vehicle tick: no 20 Hz stepping at low frame rates (2026-10-19, Claude Code; coordinator's task with the owner's phone report "sound becomes choppy below 30 fps"). Details: docs/AUDIO_RESEARCH.md §8. Evidence: phone log main checkout `local/android/phone-logs-20261019-0520/`; worktree kind-pike-df38cc `local/pace/checks/`.
- Cause [V code, V test]: the vehicles tick at 62.5 Hz and the engine/tyre mixers run 16 ms blocks, but RPM, throttle and tyre voice targets were published as one latest value per rendered frame. At 20 fps three ticks run back to back per frame, so the mixers stepped every 50 ms.
- Fix: `grid_tools::audio_pacing::Paced` (one value pushed per tick, one taken per 16 ms block; backlog skip-ahead and a bound keep the clocks together). Engine controls are queued by a FixedUpdate `publish` after `drive_tick` (changes without a tick from the frame update); audibility and the reset generation stay immediate. Tyre voice targets are queued per tick. At 60 fps the mixers see the same values as before. The tyre mixer skips mixing for silent voices (output unchanged).
- The original [O/I]: audio parameters are written once per rendered frame and its world steps once per frame, so no ticks are skipped; OpenAL mixes on its own thread. No extra smoothing was added.
- Load [V, desktop]: probe `grid-audio-load` (grid-probes): 12 engines + 12 tyre mixers take about 10 % of one desktop core (slowest 10 ms callback 1.9 ms); not measured on the phone.
- Diagnostic `GRID_FRAME_CAP_HZ` sleeps per frame to reproduce a slow device on the desktop.
- Identities [V], measured with `grid-opponent-race ROOT CD01 BP01 1 11200 ai` / `12 11200 ai` (DF01 and characteristics unset) on 673035e plus F452/F453: solo c770ff7f…be06, 12-car ae904918…b7c0, equal to F447 (audio never writes physics). robustness reports 6bc60af8…2cd6 / 8f932cff…181f / d5eec9ba…1103 on b165167, apparently with a different invocation; not reconciled here.
- Tests: `a_slow_frame_reaches_the_mixer_one_tick_per_block`, `audio_pacing` units; the existing engine pause/mute/reset test passes.
