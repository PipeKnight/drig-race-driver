F936 Phone GPU batch 2: texture mip skip at upload (mipskipN_M) (2026-11-26, Claude Code).
- Asked by the coordinator for the owner: can the phone drop the top mip levels of large textures to cut GPU bandwidth and VRAM. New perf-test word `mipskipN` / `mipskipN_M` (N = 1..3 levels, M = minimum largest side, default 0), read once before the first upload (`texture_upload::mip_skip`), off by default, so no default changes on any platform. Mechanism, exclusions and the phone plan: docs/ANDROID_GPU.md section 10, "Batch 2: texture mip skip".
- The skip is general: every world texture (track, cars, sky, props) whose largest side is ≥ M and whose chain is longer than N. Kept whole: UI and front-end images (new `image_ui` / `image_linear_ui` at the OSD, results art, loading stats, livery carousel and FE world call sites), arrays, cube maps, 3D textures, images without RENDER_WORLD, and any size that would stop being whole BC blocks. A shrunk image keys its ETC2 cache entry by its own levels. Car parts and the damage model are untouched; only texel data shrinks.
- The texture-upload summary gains `mip_skip=N_M mip_skipped=<images> mip_skipped_bytes=<bytes>`, and the perf-test label gains `,mipskipN_M`, so the phone's auto-run rows name the variant.
