F942 Phone mip skip: owner's combined look (mipskip2_1024+1) with a 64 px floor (2026-11-26, Claude Code).
- Owner decision (2026-11-26, via the coordinator, after the F936 before/after sheets), verbatim: "на глаз вообще не видно разницы, смело можно брать такую версию для телефонов. Думаю, что без 2 мипмапов на крупных текстурах и без 1 на остальных - норм!" (by eye there is no visible difference; for phones, two mips off the large textures and one off the rest is fine).
- `MipSkip` rules now combine: `mipskip2_1024+1` (or the words `mipskip2_1024,mipskip1`) drops two levels from world textures of ≥ 1024 texels and one from the rest; each texture takes the rule with the largest minimum it reaches. A later rule with the same minimum replaces an earlier one. Label and summary print the rules (`mipskip2_1024+1_0`, `mip_skip=2_1024+1_0`).
- New floor `MIP_SKIP_FLOOR` = 64: the skip drops fewer levels (or none) rather than leave a texture's shorter side under 64 texels; a texture without mips is never shrunk. General for every track and car, no per-asset list.
- Exclusions audited: HUD, front end, livery carousel, loading statistics and results art go through `image_ui` / `image_linear_ui` (F936); HUD text comes from font files, not DDS; no LUT goes through this path (Bevy's tonemapping LUT is its own, the car-number LUT is a CPU table); cubes and arrays are kept, as is the HUD's `LightMap` cube. Track and car occlusion/light textures do shrink: low-frequency, and present in the sheets the owner judged.
- Off by default on every platform; the desktop stays pixel-identical (below). The Android default waits for the phone A/B (heavy-view GPU ms, load time, frames ≥ 50 ms) and the coordinator's check of the HUD and decal crops.
