F889 Race countdown waits for the pipeline warm-up (2026-11-23, Claude Code).
- Task (coordinator, after F884): make the race countdown wait for the pipeline warm-up to reach Done, so a slow
  PC never compiles pipelines after GO. In F884's slow derby trace run the warm-up ended after GO.
- Change: `StartHold` in `pipeline_warmup.rs`, checked at the top of `drive_tick` (main.rs). In window runs the
  fixed step skips its drive tick while no tick has run yet and the warm-up is not Done, for at most 1800 fixed
  steps (30 s at 60 Hz). The first tick, and with it the countdown, then starts. Once a tick has run it never
  holds again, so restarts and later phases are unchanged. The ticks themselves are the same; only their start
  moves. Offscreen captures and headless runs have no `Warmup` resource and never hold, so `tools/identity.tsv`
  and the fixed-step captures do not depend on it. One line per run:
  `drive_start_hold version=1 event=released held_steps=N reason=warmup_done|limit|ticking`.
  `GRID_START_AFTER_WARMUP=0` starts at once as before.
- Test: `race_start_waits_for_the_warm_up_then_never_holds_again` (waits while the warm-up runs, starts on Done,
  never holds after a tick or a release, starts after the limit if the warm-up never ends).
- Evidence (windowed derby, Enhanced 1280, the A/B runs of F887; the countdown events follow the release in
  every run):
  - `local/tick/mk-a-1`: warm-up Done at frame 298, 10745 ms; `held_steps=631 reason=warmup_done`; then
    countdown 4, 3, 2, 1, GO.
  - The other nine runs (mk-a-2, mk-b-1/2, sp-a-1..3, sp-b-1..3): Done at 2218-11280 ms, held 118-668 steps,

  - Observation: a cold start held 10-11 s (pipelines compiled from an empty cache), a warm one about 2 s.
