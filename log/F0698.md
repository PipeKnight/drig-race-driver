F698 Car glass: transparent items sort by their current position; the windscreen no longer shows over the rear window (2026-11-02, Claude Code).
- Task: the coordinator's owner report (Viper, chase view, prototype dc4408b43366, screenshots 36–38): sometimes the front windscreen and its frame show through the rear window, contrasty and sharp; gone at one angle, back at a slightly different one, also with reflections off. Backlog row 106.
- Cause [observed]: Bevy 0.19.1 `queue_material_meshes` stores a blended item's world centre when it queues the entity (`add_retained`) and never updates it while the entity moves; `sort_phase_system` recomputes only the view distance from that point. Car glass writes no depth, so whichever pane draws last covers the other. Window-phase audits (1280 × 800 Baseline, reflections off, build 85e1c69c): Washington standstill after spawn, centres exact and the windscreen 313 before the rear window 310; 500 ticks straight, centres 142.7 m stale, order still right; Donington, Viper circling 1,250 ticks, centres 15–18 m stale and the rear window sorted FIRST at all three camera offsets, the cracked windscreen drawn crisply over it (as the owner's 38). Codex's G237/G238/G313 found the same stale centres on the Lacetti and refreshed them only for the player's windows in Donington Lacetti driving.
- Fix: `mesh_stream::resort` writes every mesh item's centre each frame in every view, from the entity's current world-from-local and the asset's (or the streamed) local centre. The original sorts its instances each frame (`carglass` `instancesRequireSorting` 1; each pane is its own instance). Covers all cars, opponents, lamps, the reflection overlays.
- Not ported: the original's `carglass` / `car_light_glass` depth writes (LEQUAL, on); two-sided glass already matches. Docs: PERFORMANCE.md "World sort centres of moving meshes (F698)", LACETTI_GRAPHICS_MATERIALS.md note on G313, FIDELITY_BACKLOG row 106.
