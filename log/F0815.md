  - Paint: metal mask m = sat((m1.a − 0.3)·8); albedo ×= 1 + m·(sat(n·v + falloff) − 1); C2 = C1 + k·(M − C1) with k = pow(sat(sun·r), metallic power)·s·m1.a and M = m·sun·(diffuse·scale + 0.5); then + sun·s·strength·m1.a·pow(sat(sun·r), highlight power).
  - Window glass has the shadow gate env ×= sat(s + `SpecularValues.w`), with s = 1 on back faces. Its occlusion term is sat(1 + `SpecularValues.z`) with no map, i.e. constant 1, so the port adds only the 0.5 shadow floor. The highlight is half strength. The floors come from 0xA135C0 and are submitted by 0x9E7360; both rows are already in symbols.tsv.
