Static data and headless runs only; the original was not launched.
- Method: new probes `grid-barrier-sweep` (rays every 4 m on both sides of each race route's centreline against the port's collision world and the visible ornaments) and `grid-barrier-impact` (the app's collision world, AI lap, a clone steered into each side every 250 m; Lacetti 30 m/s and R10 70 m/s on the 90 race routes).
- Le Mans [O]: the boundary sheet is continuous and held in 698 branches (deepest 2.25 m at 62 m/s). The visible armco stood a median 1.1 m (up to ~2.6 m) in front of it with no collision of its own [I: the likely "flew through the fences"].
- Root cause [V/D]: `entities::read` skipped `TEMPLATEBASICENTITYINSTANCE`: 69,014 placements on the race routes (fences, concrete, armco, ad boards, buildings, signs), 2,558 infinite-mass template shapes. The original builds them at level setup: 0x7D97B0 lists them (world +0xBBCC), 0x7D6BA0 calls 0x7CB440 per instance, which creates the body and each shape's collision primitive (box 0xA72CF0). Their meshes are the `ornaments.bin` copies, so the port drew barriers it never collided with.
- Fix: `entities::read_basic`; `Props::read_route` adds the basic entities' non-phantom shapes as fixed boxes after the entity boxes. They replace F284's authored ornament stand-ins (`ornament_barriers` and its mesh-extent code removed; FIDELITY_BACKLOG row 17 closed). The authored box-edge sanity bound grows from 200 m to 1 km (`MAX_EDGE`): Shibuya's bridge decks are up to 315 m. The spawn-press diagnostic counts basic boxes like entity boxes.
