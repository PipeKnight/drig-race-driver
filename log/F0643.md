F643 Tyre smoke rendering cost measured (SMOKE_RENDER_COST.md) (2026-10-30, Claude Code).
- Owner request: research what smoke costs to draw and how to optimise it (after F640, backlog row 97). Details: docs/SMOKE_RENDER_COST.md.
- Method: Codex's G329 Derby fixture, offscreen and deterministic: STK, Detroit route_8, 11 AI, chase_close, 900 ticks of donuts, 86 dense frames of up to 1324 sprites.
  - Source 5a70c4d2 plus an uncommitted, env-gated diagnostic patch: per-frame GPU pass times and fragment counts, a span on the G329 replay pass, and overdraw statistics with hide/unlit/near-cull switches.
  - Run on an RTX 4070 under Vulkan, release build through the build lock. Done by a subagent of the coordinator session.
- Measured, smoke pass, dense frames, mean/max:
  - 3024x1890: 8.0–8.6/18 ms; 1920x1080: 3.0–3.1/6.2 ms. With smoke not drawn: 0.04 ms.
  - Unlit material: −44/−46 %. Dropping sprites within 3 m: −17/−22 %.
  - Before G329 (`--smoke-boundary off`): the same as G329 within noise.
  - Overdraw: 51–54 screens on average, 105 at most, mostly from sprites 2–15 m away. The fragment counter shows 197 M fragments on average and 420 M at peak (correlation 0.915 with coverage).
  - Cost scales 2.78x for 2.76x the pixels.
  - CPU: `tyre_smoke::draw` 0.3 ms per frame. The G329 render-world systems take 0.27 ms per frame even without smoke, mostly `live.rs` `extract` scanning every `Mesh3d` five times.
- Interpretation: the owner's MacBook drop is smoke fill cost. An M4 at roughly 1/5–1/7 of the 4070 gives 60–125 ms per frame, matching the 60–177 ms frames seen. The M4 ratio comes from public specs, not a measurement.
- Ranked options:
  1. Reduced-resolution smoke buffer, as the original's pfx_half_smoke: about −70 % at half per axis, estimated, not run.
  2. Per-particle lighting instead of per-pixel PBR: −45 % upper bound, measured.
  3. Near-camera fade, only as a safety valve.
  4. G329 extract caching the five group entities: −0.2 ms CPU (Codex's file).
  5. The high-DPI render-scale default, done in F642.
- Not measured: windowed frame time, shadow/post passes, MSAA1 vs MSAA4, Metal/Android, the reduced-resolution prototype.
- Evidence (private): local/smoke-cost/ in the main checkout, 18 MB (diag.patch, run.ps1, analyze*.py, runs/*/stdout.log, draft). The subagent worktree .claude/worktrees/agent-aacd1868a4abe89ba still holds the patch applied; prune its build dir before removing it.
