- Found [V static]:
  - The AI packet lives at +8 of the "ai" input device (vtable 0xCF7218); device slots +4/+8 return it.
  - The per-frame input stage 0x828670 sends [0] to channels 1/9 and [1] to 2/10.
  - Then the gear: if +0x1E == 1, it calls 0x98E190(car, 9), i.e. gear +196C = 9 (refused while +21F2 is set). Otherwise, if the gear is 9 or 0 and packet +0x24 == 3, it sets gear 1.
  - Then 0x98F110(car+18C8, +0x24). The AI writes +0x24 = 3 every tick (0x794436): automatic + clutch, **no brake-to-reverse**.
  - The packet getter 0x7EE890 has no caller; only 0x8289EB of the 40 byte reads of +0x1E is car-side.
- Port:
  - `Runtime::set_ai_packet(reverse)` is a one-tick record applied in `stage_driver` (mode 3 + the gear rule). Without a packet, the car's own mode is reapplied each tick; this is unchanged for every existing car and correct after an autopilot hands back.
  - `AiDriver` publishes plain |throttle|/brake/handbrake and `reverse_request()`.
  - The AUTHORED pedal swap (REVERSE_SELECT_*, `View::reverse_gear`) is removed.
  - Applied at every AI call site: grid-opponent-race, soak opponents, touge, multi tests, app opponents and autopilot. The soak autopilot (human stand-in) gets none.
  - The recovery reverse phases (modes 1/3/5, obstruction toggle, recover_tbone) were already ported; nothing to add.

  - In the grid-app Le Mans 40 s test, an off-track AI car in Recovery brushed a barrier at 58 m/s. The obstruction toggle requested reverse, gear 9 engaged at about 52 m/s, and the car was Out(Wrecked).
