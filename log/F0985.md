F985 Refresh pacing: the drawable wait is not frame work; re-anchor after a missed refresh (2026-11-27, Claude Code).
- Implements F981's two pacer proposals, approved by the coordinator as one general change for all platforms. Only frame timing changes: no simulation code, no render pass, no image change.
- `crates/grid-app/src/refresh_pace.rs`:
  - `frame_work(interval, slept, acquire)`: the work the pacer judges is the frame interval minus the pacing sleep and minus the last finished render frame's swap-chain acquire (`frame_split::last_acquire_ms`, measured on every frame on every platform, F932). A frame that only waited for a drawable no longer counts as work, so a presentation phase slip cannot push `work_p90_ms` over the paced period × `OVERRUN` (F981: 18.9-27.4 ms against 18.3 ms before each drop from 60 to vsync). The mean interval still has to fit the period × `KEEPS_UP`, so a frame that really runs slow (a GPU-bound frame that waits in acquire) still leaves the rate.
  - `Anchor`: when the acquire wait exceeds `ANCHOR_MS` (1 ms), the paced present missed a refresh and the drawable queue is full; the next pace deadline moves later by that wait (at most one pace period), then holds for 2 frames so the queue can drain. The pace clock thus follows the display's phase instead of drifting against it. `refresh_pace anchors=N acquire_ms=... pace_hz=...` is printed once a second when it moved.
  - Tests: `frame_work_leaves_out_sleep_and_drawable_wait`; `a_drawable_wait_does_not_leave_60` (120 Hz, 2 minutes of seconds with mean 16.9 ms and 25 ms intervals of which 10 ms acquire stay at 60; 24 ms of real work still leaves 60); `anchor_moves_the_deadline_once_per_full_queue` (waits up to `ANCHOR_MS` do not move it, 9 ms moves it 9 ms then holds two frames, 40 ms is capped at one period).
- Windows smoke (RTX 4070, the owner's 165 Hz monitor, `tools/frame_split_smoke.ps1 -Extra "refresh_pacing = on"`, muted): 44 one-second windows: the first 4 (startup, before the pacer engages) at vsync, then 40 consecutive windows paced at 82.5 Hz with no drop back to vsync; median 82.5 FPS, median acquire 0.11 ms, no re-anchor printed (the wait never exceeded `ANCHOR_MS` on this PC). Evidence `local/refresh_pace_smoke2/stdout.log`.
- 60 Hz pacing cannot be shown on this PC: a 165 Hz display offers 82.5 Hz as its only paced rate (165/3 = 55 is below `MIN_HZ`). The 60-on-120 case is covered by the unit tests; the owner measures it on the Mac.
