F934 Phone GPU batch 1: size cull, tonemap and ADPF switches, GPU clock sampler (2026-11-26, Claude Code).
- Task (coordinator, 2026-11-26): bring the phone's GPU frame (Xiaomi 13 Ultra, Adreno 740, 1026x480, 60 Hz) from about 25 ms toward 20 ms and remove the dips. Every candidate is a runtime switch, off by default, until the phone shows a gain beyond noise; the desktop draws the same pixels. Coordinator facts during the batch: the dips are whole slow seconds in heavy views (GPU 34 vs 23 ms, 2600 vs 1800 visible entities, 96 vs 62 draws); the phone throttles after about 2 minutes (GPU 23 → 27–30 ms); with a 30 FPS cap the throttled heavy views still take GPU 29.5 ms, so the target is about 26 ms or less there. Plan, ranked table and expected effects: docs/ANDROID_GPU.md section 10.
- New switches (`perf_test` words; on the mobile preset they add to `lean`):
  - `cullNNN` (`mobile::static_range`, `mobile::size_range`, `mobile::radius`): every static mesh is drawn only within NNN times its bounding radius (never nearer than 40 m, 10 % fade, distance from the box centre, `VisibilityRange` with `use_aabb`); where `far_props` (110 m) is nearer for a small prop it wins. Car parts never get a range (`MovingPart` excluded), so damage and detachment are untouched. On 480 rows and a 60° view, `cull200` hides meshes under about 4 px, `cull100` under 8 px. General for every track: the range follows each mesh's own size. Restore keeps the previous range per entity (`ranged: HashMap<Entity, VisibilityRange>`), so turning the word off puts back `far_props` or nothing.
  - `tonemap`: the 3D cameras draw with `Tonemapping::None` and `DebandDither::Disabled` (without HDR Bevy tonemaps in every PBR fragment through a 3D LUT); restore puts back each camera's own values. A cost probe; the picture changes.
  - `adpf` (new `crates/grid-app/src/adpf.rs`, Android only): an `APerformanceHint` session (API 33, looked up with `dlsym`) for the main, render and pool threads (tids collected over 120 frames, at most 16), target 16.67 ms; each frame reports the interval, and from API 35 `reportActualWorkDuration2` also the main-thread work and the newest GPU span (`frame_split::last_gpu_ns`). Logs `adpf version=1 event=collecting|open|report|close`; an older phone logs `result=unsupported`. Only hints change.
- `tools/android_bench.ps1`: a phone-side shell loop reads `/sys/class/kgsl/kgsl-3d0` (gpuclk, busy %, thermal_pwrlevel, devfreq cur_freq, temp; governor and max clock once) every second into `gpu-clock.txt`, read-only; the run prints `gpu_clock: samples= mhz_p10= mhz_median= mhz_p90= busy_mean= thermal_pwrlevel_max= governor= max_mhz=` (or `unreadable`). It separates a governor-held low clock from a real per-pixel cost.
