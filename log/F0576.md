- Change: new `crates/grid-app/src/static_batch.rs`, every build. `GRID_STATIC_BATCH=0` turns it off; `GRID_STATIC_BATCH_CELL` changes the cell for measurements.
  - Static track parts are merged at `PostStartup`, after `track_surface::attach`, into one mesh per material, vertex layout and 64 m cell. Vertices go into the cell frame as the vertex shader would place them, with winding kept. Only Opaque and Mask materials, at most 65,536 vertices per batch. The originals are despawned.
  - Intact breakable props get their own batches. Their entities stay without a mesh and keep the visibility set by `dynamic_props::present` and the replay; a batch drops a hidden prop's triangles and restores them when it shows again.
  - Hooks: `track_surface::attach`, `Source` and `SurfaceMaterial` are now `pub(crate)`; `main.rs` installs the module. `asset_counts.rs` reports a `batch` kind and classifies moving parts and pieces before track parts.
  - The first build also batched the player's car: its parts carry `SourcePart`. That gave 12 % changed pixels. Moving and opponent parts are now excluded.
