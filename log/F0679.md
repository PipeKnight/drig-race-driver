F679 Car reflections: the cube was black since F676 (faces not drawn); masked strength checked against the original (2026-11-02, Claude Code).
- Owner's playtest (coordinator's report): practically no reflection with or without the paint gates, black head and tail lamps, glass flickering darker and lighter. Logs (worktree local/playtest/reflections-*.log) show level original active in both runs.
- Root cause: F676's per-view "cut" set the faces to `CameraOutputMode::Skip` and copied their view textures. Bevy's `prepare_view_targets` gives a camera with no output attachment and a clear colour no view target and skips it, so the six faces were never drawn: the cube stayed black (a copy probe found no face with a view target over 60 frames; the cube dump never fired). Every reflection mixed toward black, which darkened the lamps' glass and pods; F676's halved cost was the faces not rendering.
- Fix (car_reflections.rs): the faces write their own targets again and `copy_faces` copies them into the cube, as in F670; the doc comment records why. Cube dump written again; reflections visible in the chase view.
- Lamps: with the cube working the lamp glass reflects, not black; with reflections off they are unchanged. Not a regression of the default path.
- Glass flicker: not reproduced. Rear-window brightness over 30 captured frames (chase view, ticks 200–260) has the same single dip with reflections off and on (the start gantry). [I] The playtest's darker/lighter glass was the black reflection weighted by Fresnel as the view moved. Owner to retest.
