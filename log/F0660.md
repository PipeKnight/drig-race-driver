F660 Car reflections: the original's environment map and the plan (2026-10-31, Claude Code).
- Backlog row 100 (owner playtest F657), handed to Claude render by the coordinator; step 1 of the approved plan. Details: docs/CAR_REFLECTIONS.md; symbols rows 0x5BCA80, 0x5BCC40, 0x5BD0A0, 0x5BD1C0, 0x5C1880, 0x602FE0.
- Original (static, master Ghidra project read-only, private pseudocode in local/re-cache/):
  - Two cube targets, `dynamic_env_map` (near: clip 300 m, cull 15 m) and `dynamic_env_map_far` (clip 1000 m, cull 600 m), from 0x602400.
  - `faces` faces per frame, round robin. Every shipped option above low uses 6, so the whole cube is redrawn every frame; face 3 (−Y) has no objects.
  - Size 256, 512 at ultra; low (faces 0) binds the track's static environment map.
  - Centred on the focused car plus 0.5 m (0x5FBC10); one cube for all cars.
- Port state: Codex's `scene_probe` renders six full-scene Bevy cameras (enhanced Lacetti/Donington only).
  - Its cost could not be isolated: Bevy's per-pass GPU diagnostics merge same-named passes across cameras. Runs: RTX 4070, Vulkan, 3840x2160, 1800 ticks, off/256/1024, all exit cleanly, private local/reflections/runs/.
  - Bevy gives every active camera its own sun cascades but skips lights outside a view's render layers [O, bevy_light/bevy_pbr 0.19.1]. The port's probe will therefore use its own layer with a shadowless sun, and time itself.
- Plan (coordinator approved 31 October 2026), one push each:
  - (2) probe in the common build with levels off / original 256 / high 512 / ultra 1024 with finer LOD;
  - (3) per-material constants read from each car's saved `carpaint_metallic`/`carglass` instances, plus cracked/smashed glass;
  - (4) owner crops at 1080p and 4K before any default.
  - The phone stays at 256 unless measured cost forces 128.
