F609 Grid start: opponent car levels by distance as in the original (2026-10-27, Claude Code, focused-torvalds; coordinator relaying the owner, step 2 of the race-start FPS series after F600).
- Change (grid-app, new `car_lod.rs`, `car_batch.rs`, `main.rs` `low_lod`):
  - each opponent gets the low file's levels 2..5 as `LodPart { level }` parts on a hidden layer, with its LOD0 parts' materials (livery, occlusion); body parts deform with the car (`OpponentDeforming`);
  - car_batch merges each level per car and material, and each frame picks a level per opponent as 0x9F42A0 does: FOV-scaled distance bands, off-screen sort weight 1.5/2.5, caps nearest first, no hysteresis; race set "high" read from `cars/carlodsettings.xml`;
  - hidden batches and loose parts are not posed (`Unposed`); a level's loose parts are posed one frame before it is shown;
  - the player, models without a low file, and opponents with a LOD0 part off its batch stay at LOD0; `GRID_CAR_LOD=0` turns it off.
- Probe: `grid-car-lod-draws` (grid-probes) lists draws per LOD bundle: BMW LOD0 23,105 triangles, LOD2..5 7,548 / 4,018 / 1,546 / 494.
