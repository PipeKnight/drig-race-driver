F776 Night lights: street lights, night shader term and car headlight beams on every night route (2026-11-10, Claude Code).
- Ported the original's NightLights pass as one shared mechanism driven by track and car data (docs/NIGHT_LIGHTS.md). Static analysis only; grid.exe was not run.
  - Street lights: `tracks/*/street_lights.xml` (parser 0x732970) → `grid_formats::street_lights`, `grid_tools::street_lights::load`. Shipped data: Le Mans 162 lights (range 5.7..54.4, brightness 0.8..4, one pointing up), Haruna 4 (range 20, brightness 5). Every route of a track with the file gets them; Spa and the others have none.
  - Headlights: every car slot gets a beam (0x736020): weight 1.5 × clamp((F − d_i)·4), range 110 focus / 60 others, beam normalize(0, −0.45 timeline / −0.8 forced night, 0.9962).
  - Shader term (NightLights fragments): rectangle gate, sat(1 − |v|/range), facing sat((1 − d)N·L + d), cookie halves from `headlights.pssg`, weight 3 × min(w, 1); summed, saturated, × lightColour × lightStrength, added to the diffuse light. Implemented as a patched `bevy_pbr` spot-light loop (pinned to the Bevy 0.19.1 hashes) with marked shadowless spot lights and clustered-decal cookies (`crates/grid-app/src/night_lights.rs`, `night_lights.wgsl`).
  - Timing from `vehicle_lights::State` and the Le Mans timeline in driving; a static scene snaps the lamps on at its start hour (`sky::static_start`, diagnostic `GRID_SKY_HOUR`). `GRID_NIGHT_LIGHTS=off` disables the pass for A/B.
  - grid-app now enables the Bevy feature `pbr_clustered_decals`: the coordinator must refresh the build seed (`tools/build_seed.ps1`).
