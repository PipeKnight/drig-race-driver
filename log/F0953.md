The staged check runs the build's event, Donington and Washington, not only Donington as F951 says.

The marker reads "played only on cue"; the check finds it by its `# Speech lines` prefix, as before. The three lists were rewritten to match, so a run's random cue no longer reaches them. Washington's played line went back into its place in the speech section, and the speech sections are identical to before F951 (1429 lines each).
