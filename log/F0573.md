- Desktop Auto (`crates/grid-app/src/render_scale.rs`):
  - The Video screen's `Auto` now works on the desktop. Its range is native down to half of native, rounded up to 24 rows (2560 × 1369: 696–1369; `auto_range`, `Dynamic::set_range`).
  - It starts at native and stays there while frames are on time; native means no scaled image.
  - A resized window starts again at its native height (`render_dynamic version=2 event=range`).
  - The desktop default stays `native`; with the mobile preset the range is still 480–720. The help text on the Video screen says so (`front_end.rs`). Settings round-trip unchanged (`auto` parses and is saved as before).
- Controller fixes for both platforms, found by the new desktop test (a jump from 7 ms to 80 ms pixel-bound frames was probed and judged `ineffective`):
  - A step down waits for 20 consecutive late frames (`LATE_FRAMES`).
  - The probe compares a frame time smoothed by 0.25 per frame (`PROBE_SMOOTHING`) instead of 0.1. One probe second at 80 ms is 13 frames, too few for the slow average to settle.
  - The late and on-time decisions keep the 0.1 average. The F571 phone tests pass unchanged.
