F325 input abstraction and gamepad (P1, P2) (2026-10-16, Claude Code). Details are in docs/PLATFORMS_AND_INPUT_RESEARCH.md 7.1.

Every device now goes through the original action layer and the human record. The keyboard's driving output is unchanged, bit for bit.

Code:
- New grid-app input.rs:
  - the 23 original actions;
  - bindings {source, calibration}, with the calibration ported exactly from AD9AD0/AD9A80 (SSE f32);
  - the action value is the maximum over the bindings, starting at 0; "down" is threshold <= v; pressed edges as in B7C120;
  - the 827D10 record: throttle and brake clamped, handbrake > 0.5, steering SteerL - SteerR (port sign, computed as before), Gear Up/Down edges and the last direct gear.
- input::sample runs in PreUpdate after InputSystems. The frame's gear edges go into ShiftQueue (AUTHORED bound 8), and drive_tick pops one per fixed tick. This fixes the just_pressed-in-FixedUpdate double or lost shift. Pause and Look Back also go through the action layer.
- New grid-formats action_map.rs reads the ActionMap preset XML.
- [D] New finding: the binding defaults come from constructor 8400B0 (uniPos, dz 0.2, sat 0.8, threshold 0.5).
- [input] settings, from --input SPEC or an [input] section of the --assists-file:
  - device=auto|keyboard|pad|wheel; keyboard and pad give Assists.input Keyboard (+E6C 1), wheel gives Wheel, auto keeps --assists input;
  - steering/accelerate/brake deadzone and saturation overrides (654C20/81CD30); dz >= sat is refused.
- Gamepad:
  - Bevy Gamepad through gilrs 0.11.2 (WGI on Windows);
  - GamepadSettings are set to pass-through on connection (dead zones 0, change thresholds 0, no trigger rounding);
  - sticks 0.5 + 0.5x; triggers and buttons 0..1;
  - the pad bindings are read at runtime from the user's own ActionMap/actionMapPreset4.xml (schemeType xinputScheme checked; never copied) and merged with the keyboard map.
- AUTHORED device guard: gilrs maps a wheel's axes by index, so its pedals could arrive as triggers. With device=auto, only names that look like gamepads are used; wheel or joystick names and the Fanatec/Thrustmaster/Moza vendor ids are ignored. device=pad accepts any device. Each connection is logged as drive_input_pad (preset4=used|ignored(reason)).
- Rumble is not sent (stub until P4).
- Play GRID Prototype.cmd passes GRID_PLAY_INPUT as --input and lists the pad controls.
