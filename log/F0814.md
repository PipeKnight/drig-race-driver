- Traced [D]: T is 2 for every offline race (F810), so the offline layouts are the F switch of 0x6BB0C0 for T 2. 0x6B2D80 hides every group, then elements are appended; each binds a slot's `{slot}_group`/`_title`/`_time` (0x6B3760) and shows it (0x8D4670; hide 0x8D46A0). Provider slot 0x24 (index 9) is the time limit, 0x80E5E0 = max(24 h − race time × scale, 0), true only for race type 3 (Le Mans). The race time and remaining time are titled `osd_time` (TIME), not `osd_race`. The remaining time is HH:MM with the minutes rounded up (0x69DF40), MM:SS in drift freestyle (0x69DF90). The best-lap element (0x6B89C0) shows when a faster best arrives (0x9084C0 unsigned µs less-than) and, with its flag +0xA0 set as in the race layouts, hides after 5.0 s (0xCED5C8).
- Layouts (top down): F 0/1 with a time limit: remaining in `race_group`, best (popup) in `lap_group`; without: lap counter, race time, lap time, best (popup); F 2/3 lap counter; F 4 remaining MM:SS with a limit, else nothing; F 5 lap counter and race time; F 6 race time. [I] byte [+0x7C8]+0x655 (drops lap and best) taken as 0.
