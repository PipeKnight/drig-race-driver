F859 Per-frame mesh uploads: dents and car batches stream changed vertices; reflection overlays follow a part leaving its batch (2026-11-19, Claude Code).
- Mac-perf item 7: per-frame GPU uploads during a race, their bytes and render-thread prepare cost. Details and tables in docs/PERFORMANCE.md "Per-frame mesh uploads during a race (F859)".
- Probe (kept in `frame_split.rs`): each render frame takes the uploads of the main frame it draws; `frame_stats` splits `r_meshes`/`r_prepare` by frames with and without prepared meshes and reports `up_max_kb`/`stream_max_kb`. Windowed 1280x800, native height, auto_no_vsync, idle PC, Donington and the derby.
- Before: whole-mesh re-uploads from dents (opponents and the player's car) and car-batch rebuilds; derby 265 per s, 4.5 MB/s, single frames up to 1.3 MB; a frame with prepared meshes cost about 0.3 ms more `r_meshes`. Streamed senders (crowd cells, tyre smoke 7.5-23 MB/s, skid marks 3.6, ground cover 1.3, wisps 3.4-4.6 MB/s) write ranges into existing buffers and stay as they are.
- Change (shared paths, no track or car lists): `mesh_stream::replace` writes only the differing 64-vertex runs over the GPU buffer and updates the asset without a change event when the structure matches, otherwise replaces it; dents use it (batched parts keep the main-world path). `car_batch` rebuilds keep every member's vertex range and collapse members that left to zero-area triangles, so they stream through the shared `stream_changed`. Sort centres are queued per mesh.
- Root-cause fix found by the image check: `car_reflection_overlay::follow` ignored a removed `RenderLayers` (a part leaving its batch), so the overlay stayed on the hidden layer until a whole-mesh dent re-synced it by accident. It now reads `RemovedComponents<RenderLayers>`; `follow_bounds` copies a streamed part's `Aabb` to its overlay. Diagnosis by marking `Mesh3d` changed on chosen drawers (debris 240 px with streaming, 0-2 px with the re-sync).
- After: derby 11.9 uploads per s, 160 KB/s, largest frame 142 KB; Donington 6.1 per s, 37 KB. Two alternating A/B pairs: `r_meshes` 1.11/1.02 -> 0.97/0.69 ms derby, 1.15/1.05 -> 1.06/0.91 Donington; `r_prepare` 1.39/1.29 -> 1.31/1.04 and 1.62/1.46 -> 1.56/1.34 ms (local/mt/up/probep*, fix3p*).
