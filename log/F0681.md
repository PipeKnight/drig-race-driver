- Original [V static] (DISCIPLINES §4.3.1):
  - Race setup 0x669700 reads the competition format enum (mode vf 0x80, +0x4C). At 0x669C53, for 2, 3, 4, 9 and 10 it calls the flag collector 0x668670.
  - For every other format it calls 0x62FEB0. That walks the entity instances (world +0xBBFC) and, for each one whose name contains `close_drift_flag` (strstr 0xA66880), calls 0x7D4B00.
  - 0x7D4B00 frees the entity's physical parts (0x7C5190: the shape leaves the world), links (0xAD12B0) and constraints (0x7D3130). `remove_joint` 0xAD9260 is the pool release under all three.
  - Formats 9 and 10 have no name in the table 0xDB39CC [O].
  - Not established: whether the flags are still drawn. 0x7D4B00 does not touch the render instance [O], so the port keeps drawing them [I].
- Port:
  - `Route::with_drift_flags` marks a drift-format event: grid-app `--discipline drift_*` and `grid-opponent-race --discipline`.
  - On any other route, `entity_geometry::entity_in_event` leaves those instances out of `Props::read_route` (no intact or damaged-base boxes) and out of `dynamic_props::Catalog::read`.
  - Before, every non-drift event on the 32 routes with flags had them as obstacles: Istanbul 1/3, Jarama 0/1, Nürburgring 0/1/3, Haruna 0, Okutama 1/2/4/5, Shibuya 0-3, Yokohama Docks 0-9, Long Beach 1/4, Washington 2/3/7/8.
