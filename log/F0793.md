- Findings [V] (docs/CAREER_RESEARCH.md §14.2):
  - 0x660E90 (`player_team_position`) keys the player and the teammate (car types 1, 2) as 0. An AI car's key is `team.id` of its team override (car +0x14) or its driver's `team_id`, read with 0x81AAD0 (`db_field_get`), which writes the output only on a hit; the key starts at 0. A car without a driver or team, or whose team has no row, therefore has key 0 and adds to the player's sum. The new sum entry's player flag is `key == 0`.
  - Le Mans fields (0x663860) give every AI car its `lemans_restrictions.driver_id`, so they follow the same rule.
  - [D] In the shipped data no career AI car reaches key 0: the 1328 `event_driver`, `event_legend` and `lemans_restrictions` rows all name a driver with a team row, and none of the 250 team rows has id 0.
  - [I] The car fields +0x0C and +0x14 are read only from this routine.
