F759 Circuit lap estimator 0x67FFB0 and marshal flags (rows 44, 47) (2026-11-09, Claude Code).
- Row 44, ported: the circuit-race lap estimator 0x67FFB0 (virtual race, constructor 0x67D590) predicts the laps of each car still racing when a race is ended early, lap by lap from the world-record base, the car's time and lap fraction, its previous lap and an MT19937 draw. `grid_tools::virtual_race::LapEstimator` / `circuit_estimator`, `race::Standings::with_estimator` (finalize 0x90FCD0 → `estimate_laps`), wired in grid-app `build_race` and `grid-opponent-race`. Static RE and port notes: docs/RACE_RULES_RESEARCH.md §1 "Lap estimator".
- Row 47, ported: marshal flags (rules system 0x94A4A0, flag system 0xD10D6C). race_start raises the green flag once when the leader is within 20 m of the line. serious_incident (0x94AC60, 0x94A6E0, test 0x949B40) raises a yellow per car that is more than 90° off the line below 40 m/s, or below 3 m/s, for 0.5 s, after 10 s of race and at least 8 s after that car's last yellow; the demolition format has none. end_race raises the chequered flag. Zones are 100 m before and 20 m after a yellow (green and chequered 400/100), durations 10/30/600 s, and the rules tick every 0.3 s. `crates/grid-tools/src/race_flags.rs` (`RuleSet` read from gameconfig/rules.xml, `RaceFlags::step`). docs/RACE_RULES_RESEARCH.md §3 "Flag system".

The port played it for the chequered flag; now `RaceView::yellow_flag`, and the debug text reads "Yellow flag". Green and chequered have no HUD element on this path.
