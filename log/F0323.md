F323 failure containment: one car's tick failure retires that car, and the race goes on (S3: R02, R16, R09) (2026-10-16, Claude Code). Details are in docs/RUNTIME_LIMITS_AUDIT.md S3 "Status (F323)". Before this change, any Err from one car's stage inside MultiRuntime::tick propagated with `?`. grid-app then paused all 12 cars, and R threw the race away.

Now MultiRuntime::tick contains each car's own stages: controls (staged_car), the vehicle manager, the car's track contacts and car-pair hull contacts inside the geometry provider, body events, and lap timing. A failing car keeps its previous state. From then on its world body is held at the last committed kinematics: body words 0x1C0..0x230 (mass centre, pose, angular and linear velocity), with both velocities zeroed. The held car still collides with the others. Its static contacts are skipped, and its Runtime::ticks clock keeps running. MultiRuntime exposes retirement(i) (tick and error) and newly_retired(). Shared stages (the world advance, prop pieces) still fail the whole tick atomically, as before. This is AUTHORED. The nearest original behaviour is skipping an over-limit island for a step (B7B850 status 1), and the original has no per-car retirement.

grid-app changes:
- A failing car becomes Out::Retired in the standings. The app logs one `drive_car_retired` line and the HUD shows "NAME retired: reason", or "Your car retired: reason. R restarts" for the player. The field races on. A retired car's engine throttle is 0.
- A world-level pause now shows "Simulation paused: tick N: ..." in the HUD instead of the generic text.
- A refused time-trial tick no longer pauses. It logs `drive_trial_event type=refused`, and the new grid-world State::skip_tick counts the lap as an invalid attempt and keeps the trial clock in step. When no lap is still timed, the runtime's current lap is marked invalid.
- A refused player dent projection keeps the previous mesh, as the opponent path does. One `drive_body_projection event=refused` line is logged per refusal kind, and the field is not projected again until it changes.
- Rate limiting: race reports count only the player's own body records (new MultiRuntime::body_contacts_of), not every contact in the field. The after-finish AI's continuous controls and a retired player's contacts no longer trigger a report.
- New `log_line!` macro (writeln! to a locked stdout, error ignored). It replaces println! on the per-tick and per-event paths: Driving::report, lap/trial/race-result/retirement lines, and damage, prop-break and audio events. Startup prints are unchanged.
