- Cause [V port]: Armco rails, tyre walls and flags keep their damage geometry in one skinned mesh per renderable, each vertex weighted over up to four joints (DYNAMIC_PROPS_RESEARCH "Skinned damage meshes"). grid-app drew it split per joint (each triangle rigid with its main joint, AUTHORED since F566). Once the rail's pieces move apart, triangles that straddle joints separate: the strips.
- Fix:
  - grid-formats: `Renderables::skins_in(renderable, anchors)` gives each skinned instance with, per joint, its transform to the root, its inverse bind matrix and the piece anchor it is at or under. `skin_draws` (the per-joint split, still used by `draws_in` and grid-tools) is rebuilt on the same reader. Root and anchor lookups are shared helpers.
  - grid-app `dynamic_props`: each skinned instance is one part (`dynamic/piece/b/s/source/id`, so it is hidden, lean-disabled and unbatched like the other pieces), a Bevy skinned mesh with the stored joints and weights; its per-joint draws are skipped. `tag` gives it one joint entity per skin joint at the intact world pose (placement × joint-to-root); `present` moves the joints of live breakables as the piece parts move (now × start⁻¹ × rest) and restores a breakable's joints once when it stops being live. `Car::piece_skins` → resource `PieceSkins`.
  - Donington: 831 skinned meshes (`dynamic_props ... skinned_meshes=831`), drawn pieces and `pieces_without_draws` unchanged (14,463 / 0).
