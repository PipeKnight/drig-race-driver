- Original [V, decompile cache]:
  - The HUD manager's Play 0x43E770 `(effect, a2, a3)`. With a3 = 0 it walks the 20 slots in order: a slot that holds this effect drops the play, and a free slot forgets its effect. With a3 = 1 the effect may overlap itself.
  - The wrapper 0x440E10 adds the `low_` prefix in a race.
  - The callers are the list widget 0x490F40 (UDLR_move a3 = 1, select a3 = 0), the itemflow 0x49DA70 (item_discrete 0x474100, dead_end 0x474130), the slider 0x4A0CC0, the screen enter and leave whooshes 0x4799E0 and 0x479A10, back 0x479930, and the overlay on/off sounds 0x49CFA0, 0x4936A0 and 0x4942F0.
  - In a race every `low_` variant, `low_back` included, plays an `ah_*` variety at −4.4 dB.
  - Details are in docs/AUDIO_RESEARCH.md §36; 11 new symbols rows.
- Port (general, every screen through shared code):
  - `grid_tools::hud_audio` loads the front-end set (`Bank::load_front_end`) next to the race's `low_` set, and `Player::play` follows the a3 rule.
  - `hud_sound::install_front_end` is called by each screen app after `transition::install`. It plays `screen_whoosh_in` on the first frame and `screen_whoosh_out` once `Fade::leaving()` is true. It only reads `Fade`; the F767 transition code is unchanged.
  - The event menu, the pause menu and the pre-race screens queue the widget cues (move, value step, dead end, select, back) from their step functions, and a drain plays them.
  - Deviations [I]: the pre-race screens use the `low_` set; the sliders play item_discrete; mouse clicks play the key cues; the overlays (popups) are not ported, so overlay_on/off have no caller (§36).
- Already settled and only referenced: wind is never started in the original (§26, F583), and the OSD countdown beeps are online-only (§28, F589).
- Speech race history (SPEECH_AUDIO_RESEARCH §19, SPEECH_CAREER_PARAMETERS):

  - So `firstWinEver` now plays at the first career win, and `wonChampionshipFirstTime` only at the first championship. Before, it played at every championship win.
