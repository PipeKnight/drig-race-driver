  - weight × sat(2·m1.a): each car's own `main_m1` (an opponent its livery's), uploaded linear;

  - Only `carpaint_metallic` parts take them; glass and lamps keep F670's response.
  - [I] SpecularValues.z/.w taken as 0: the only setter found, 0x9E7360, is the debug material editor (symbols row added). OcclusionMapScale as saved, 1 when absent.
  - No gate fades downward-facing surfaces; the earlier note was a misreading. Dust, scratch and damage vertex masks are 1 on a clean car and are not ported.

  - Look: the over-mirror paint is gone; the livery colours read as with reflections off, with a glossy environment sheen; paint in shadow mixes toward a dark environment (private local/reflections/out/mask-check-1080.png).
- Per-view cost (profile build, `span_profile`, private runs prof2-*): for six faces about 3.0 ms finishing one wgpu command buffer per render-graph system per face, 1.7 ms queue submit, 0.7 ms visibility, 0.8 ms preprocessing setup. Three of those systems repeat global indirect-parameter work once per view; that order is Bevy's.
- Cut: faces skip their output pass (`CameraOutputMode::Skip`); `copy_faces` copies each drawn view's own texture into the cube.
  - Quiet runs, baseline 1080p, render schedule CPU: off 4.4–5.4 ms; original before 13.2–14.9 ms, after 8.0–9.5 ms; high 8.7–9.5; ultra 8.5. So six faces cost about 0.65 ms each instead of 1.5.
  - Loaded runs of both builds scatter to 19–21 ms; runs with the main schedule above 4.1 ms are excluded.
- Option, not default: `GRID_CAR_REFLECTIONS_FACES=N` redraws N faces per frame in turn (the original's round robin); 3 / 2 / 1 faces measured 7.4 / 6.9 / 5.5 ms.
- Batching fix (car_batch.rs, ornament_lod.rs, car_reflections.rs): with reflections on, the probe layer {0, 27}, `assign_layers` inserting {0} on every mesh, and the overlay children stopped all car batching (0 of 613 parts batched; opponents drawn part by part without their shadow body's shadow; ornament levels and the probe re-set each other's layers every frame). Now `car_reflections::default_layers` treats no layers, {0} and {0, 27} alike for batching and ornament levels; overlays do not count as a part's children; a mesh whose layers are removed is rechecked at once. Verified: 613 of 613 parts in 104 batches with reflections on, as off; the opponent BMW's ground shadow is back (private out/chk2-opp.png). The F670 costs and the before/after numbers above were all measured with batching broken; the cut's relative gain stands, the absolute "on" costs must be re-measured.
