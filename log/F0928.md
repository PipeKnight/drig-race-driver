- Task (coordinator; owner 2026-11-25: "Нам не страшно менять картинку на телефоне... это ок, если там будет меньше деталей или хуже качество картинки"): make lean the Android default in shared code, the desktop unchanged; then the 120 Hz pacing A/B (backlog row 153).
- Change: the mobile preset's render profile is `lean` (F927's set of switches: no MSAA, 256 px sun shadow map within 4/25 m with a 2x2 hardware filter, no shadows from small props and their draw distance cut to 110 m, no car reflection overlays, no point or spot lights).
  - `perf_test::PerfTest` has a `base` (`base_for(mobile::enabled())`: LEAN with the preset, NORMAL otherwise); the screen's, the setting's and the Auto cycle's switches add to it (`PerfTest::active()`), as do `car_reflections` and the cockpit mirror.
  - The word `full` (`[diagnostics] perf_test = full`, `android_bench.ps1 -PerfTest full`) starts from NORMAL, for A/B runs on the phone.
  - Gated by `mobile::enabled()` (Android, or `GRID_MOBILE_PRESET=1` on the PC), not by device. `mobile_preset version=3` logs `render_profile=lean` with the switch label.
  - Car parts, damage and detachment are untouched: the profile changes shadow casting, visibility ranges of small static props (not car parts: `MovingPart` is excluded), the camera's MSAA and filter, lights and overlays.
- Pacing logs (Android only): `android_frame_rate` adds `modes_hz` (the display's supported mode rates); new `android_display_hz` line, read once a second and logged when the rate changes, since the rate read right after `setFrameRate` may lag the switch. `android_bench.ps1` prints both, and `mobile_preset`.
