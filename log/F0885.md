F885 Dynamic resolution: no ineffective jump from vsync-quantized frames, holds verified (Mac M4) (2026-11-23, Claude Code).
- Defect (coordinator, from the owner's MacBook M4 log, build f8b67a22):
  - The probe 1056 -> 984 rows measured `probe_gain_ms=1.27` against `pixel_gain_ms=2.61`. Its frames were 19.8 and 18.5 ms, just above `LATE_MS` 18 and the 16.7 ms refresh.
  - `Dynamic::update` called it `ineffective`, set the 1440 ceiling and held 60 s. The Mac then ran 2523x1440 at 30-44 fps for the rest of the race (acquire 20-28 ms, render 22-33, main 4-5, r_encode about 2.5), with no step down allowed.
  - The `from` height is inferred: 2.61 / 19.8 gives (984/from)^2 = 0.868, so from = 1056. The log itself is not in this checkout.
- Interpretation: a display synchronised to its refresh shows frames at 16.7 ms or one period later. An 18-20 ms mean counts missed refreshes, which does not follow the pixel count. The probe measured vsync, and the F571 rule then jumped to a ceiling that earlier probes had left for good reason.
- Fix, shared by every platform (`crates/grid-app/src/render_scale.rs`; log `render_dynamic version=4`, with `near_ms` on the install line):
  - (b) Expected gain is capped at `before - TARGET_MS`. A step whose frames are at or below `LATE_MS` stays. A step from frames below `NEAR_MS` = 1.25 x 16.7 ms that gains at least `NEAR_SHARE` (0.25) of the expected gain is inconclusive.
  - (a) An ineffective probe returns to the probe's start height, not the ceiling. On the first probe that is still the ceiling, so F571 is unchanged.
  - (c) The hold records the probed height, its frame time and the expected gain. From one second after the return until the hold ends: if the held height runs late and slower than the probed one by more than the whole expected gain, the controller goes back to the probed height (`reason=verify_late`), blocks the held height for 15 s and ends the hold.
  - (c) is not taken literally ("never hold at a late height"): the phone's CPU-bound race is late at every height and must still hold at 720 (F571).
- Test `dynamic_height_does_not_hold_where_frames_run_late` (Mac replay):
  - The probe state 1056 -> 984 at 19.8/18.5 ms keeps 984. The gain reproduces 1.27 against 2.61, and nothing is judged `ineffective`.
  - The old hold at 1440 (probe of 984 at 17.5 ms) with 25/33 ms frames returns to 984 by `verify_late`, with under 2 s late at 1440 and the hold cleared.
  - A 120 s race from the ceiling spends under 4 s late at 1440, warm-up included.
