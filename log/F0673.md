F673 Doppler for the opponent engines and the crowd loops: the game's own CMStream_Update shift (backlog row 71) (2026-10-31, Claude Code, car-series; Coordinator relaying the owner, 2026-10-30/31: row 71, positional output and Doppler, root cause and 1:1 with the original).
- Research ([AUDIO_RESEARCH.md](../docs/AUDIO_RESEARCH.md) §32) [O static, master cache; the original was not run; a subagent surveyed every Doppler and velocity writer]:
  - the law: OpenAL's Doppler factor is 0, and CMStream_Update (a2cc1d–a2cd14) shifts each 3D voice itself:
    - v = pos·vel/(|pos| + 0.01), with vel = (voice − listener velocity) × the voice's Doppler property;
    - f = 343/(343 + v), or 0 at or below −343;
    - AL_PITCH = clamp(pitch·f, 0.5, 2).

    The Not3D groups are exempt.
  - Doppler and velocity per voice:
    - engine voices: the car's velocity; Doppler 1 for AI cars, 0 for the player's car outside the detached cameras;
    - ambience: 0;
    - crowd: 0.5 for loops once C+0x15A0 is set, which 0x420280 does when the race intro hands over to the in-game cameras (0x58BAF0);
    - zero-velocity voices (0x435690): 1;
    - tyres and 0x421B70 one-shots: Doppler 1 with the position-change fallback, below 0.3 %.
  - Listener velocities: the camera listener's is its displacement over the frame / dt, used only below 8 m (0x43F570). The Car listener's (0x43F7A0) is the car's velocity in Internal/Bonnet/EndOfRace, else the camera's.
  - §26 corrected: the handbrake one-shot keeps Doppler 1.
  - Row 71's "Output is mono" was stale since F492, and the direction term is 0 in the shipped game (§7.2). The row is reworded.
