- New test `breakable_pieces_draw_on_their_bodies` (replaces F564's
  `round_piece_meshes_sit_on_their_bodies`). For every piece of every breakable template on all
  routes of Nürburgring, Detroit, Le Mans and Donington it requires:
  - a draw from `draws_in` placed on its collision shape;
  - round pieces centred and filling their shape; box pieces' mesh centres inside their box;
  - a diffuse binding for each draw's material.
  On origin/main 154aca2c, 159 of 357 template pieces had no draw. After the change: 353 drawn
  and passing, plus 4 Armco main-section `section_dam_link1` posts that carry no geometry in
  objects.pssg.
- Three causes [O]:
  - the damage renderables are BUNDLENODEs, which the scene index skipped;
  - tyre-wall panels, Armco rails and the flag keep their broken geometry in a skinned mesh: a
    SKINNODE with skinTransformLocal, SKINJOINT -> JOINTNODE under each anchor, and a SKELETON
    of inverse bind matrices. `read_static_meshes` skipped those sources (18 on the four
    tracks);
  - Le Mans' blue and yellow tyre walls name their tyres `tyreNN_obj_01!0`.
- Fix:
  - `scene::Renderables` indexes BUNDLENODE roots and `!N`-suffixed NODEs;
  - BUNDLENODE composes as a transform ancestor;
  - `draws_in` falls back to the root's skins: one draw per joint under the anchor, with the
    triangles whose main joint it is, through inverse bind x joint chain;
  - new `mesh::read_object_meshes` (main joint per vertex), used by grid-app's object loader
    (main.rs, one line);
  - `decode_bind_matrix` clears float noise in the last column.
- AUTHORED approximation: each triangle follows one joint. The original blends up to four:
  tyre-wall panels blend about 10% of their vertices, Armco rails most of theirs (main weight
  down to 0.39). Exact at the bind pose, with seams when pieces separate or a rail bends. Blend
  skinning in grid-app is not done.
