F820 Front-end camera stick spring (2026-11-10, Claude Code).
- Task: coordinator item 2 after F813: trace and port the front-end camera's stick spring. Static analysis and Unicorn emulation of pure code only; grid.exe was not run. Details are in docs/FRONT_END_FLOW.md §2, "Stick spring" and "Camera noise".
- Findings [D, decompiled through the master project]:
  - 0x4A9FF0 (controller update) calls 0x47F660 unless its target name is `none`. 0x47F660 reads actions 0x2B..0x2E (`Fe View Tweak Left/Right/Up/Down`; preset 4 binds the right stick, the keyboard preset leaves them unmapped) and sends x = Right − Left, y = Up − Down (message vtable 0xCB96C0, type 0xDAACC4 via 0x4B4960).
  - The spring component (ctor 0x47E770, vtable 0xCB9B9C, handler 0x47EB80 → 0x47EAE0) clamps each to ±`spring_limit`, scales by −0.5, and 0x47B7E0 makes it the user target with lane weight 1 while |target| > `spring_threshold`.
  - 0x47B970 steps the four-lane spring in sub-steps of at most 0.1 s: v += ((ku(target − x) − cu v) w + kc(0 − x) − cc v) h; x += h v. 0x47E890 orbits the camera through 0x47C7F0 by (final_scale_y x₁, final_scale_x x₀) times the dampen weight, and sends weight × v as message 0xCB849C (purpose [I], not ported).
  - Defaults (0x47B250, 0x47E770): kc 11, cc 12, ku 15, cu 11, thresholds/limits 10.1, final scale 1, dampen 1 s. The `loading` block: 1, 2, 5, 1; 0.1; 0.35; 0.25; 0.5 s.
- Orbit order and signs [E]: 0x47C7F0 run in Unicorn 2.1.4 (the graphics-python environment) on a camera object with eye +0, target +0x10, up +0x20 = +Y, roll +0x30 = 0, for single and three mixed angle pairs. Result: the second angle about the camera's up axis, then the first about its original side axis; a positive first angle lowers an eye in front of a level target, a positive second angle turns +Z towards +X. `camera_noise::orbit` matches within about 3.4e-7; pitch-then-yaw is off by 0.36–0.76. This closes F812's [I] on the order and signs. Script kept in the worktree's ignored `local/front-end-flow/spring/orbit_emu.py`.
