F648 Collision and damage loops no longer hold after a wreck, the race end or a pause (backlog row 95) (2026-10-30, Claude Code, car-series; Coordinator relaying the owner, 2026-10-30: backlog row 95 (F639), the sound freezes in its last state after WRECKED and the race end).
- Cause [V code, test]: the scrape loops (453710/458780, every car) and the player's damage loops (engine, brake, steering, suspension; 457BE0) on the collision source get their level only from `collision_sound::tick`, once per vehicle tick, and the mixer holds the last level. After terminal damage grid-app retires the player and the field (AUTHORED), `standings.over()` holds and `drive_tick` stops, so a scrape or damage loop that played at the wreck looped at its last level until exit. The same at any race end that freezes the cars mid-scrape, during a pause and after a drive failure. The engine and tyre voices were already muted on those stops (F434); the speech, HUD and one-shots end by themselves.
- The original [O static]: no freeze. The cars keep simulating, 0x458D00 calls 457BE0 for all 20 car audio slots every frame, and 458780 sets a scrape with envelope g ≤ 0.1 to −48 dB while g halves on a slow car, so a resting wreck fades out. The +0xA6B4/[+0xA494]+0x334 flags 0x458D00 sets for a car in state 3 are the AI-car audio marks of 0x411120, not a stop. Pause: the Paused mode drops the car groups to −48 dB. Details: [AUDIO_RESEARCH.md](../docs/AUDIO_RESEARCH.md) §29.
- Fix: `collision_audio::Mixer::hush` (every loop glides to silence and keeps its place) and grid-app `collision_sound::audibility` (Update): once the ticks stop (race frozen, paused, failed: the engine and tyre condition) it sends a hush, logged `collision_audio version=2 event=hush`; the next tick's levels bring the loops back. [I] Silence stands in for the fade the original's continuing simulation gives.
