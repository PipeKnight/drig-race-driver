F564 Broken tyres drawn on their bodies and lifted out of the road (2026-10-24, Claude (session fervent-napier); Coordinator, 23 October 2026: tyre pieces sink into the road and still fall asleep in the air after F561 (owner's PC Detroit demolition derby)).
- Cause 1, presentation [V]: grid-app drew broken pieces from `Renderables::draws(anchor)`.
  That composes only the anchor's own transform and leaves out the damage renderable's nodes
  above it. Tyres were drawn about (-0.61, -0.54, 0) m off their bodies, in the piece frame:
  under the road when lying flat, beside or above their body when tilted or on edge. This likely
  explains both symptoms in the owner's screenshots.
- Fix 1: new `grid_formats::scene::Renderables::draws_in(root, node)`; grid-app `append_pieces`
  uses it with the damage renderable as root. The test `round_piece_meshes_sit_on_their_bodies`
  fails with `draws` (tyrestack_black piece 0 centre (-0.609, -0.539)) and passes with
  `draws_in`.
- Cause 2, physics [V]: a car pressing tyres pushes a hull up to 0.30 m under the road for a
  tick before the solver recovers it.
- Fix 2, AUTHORED (owner's request): `PropWorld::lift_sunk` runs after every committed step,
  after `hold_lost`, in `Runtime` and `MultiRuntime`. It lifts any piece or wheel more than
  `SINK_TOLERANCE` (0.01 m) under the road back to the tolerance through
  `continuous::apply`, and clears its downward velocity.
  - The test `pieces_pressed_by_a_car_stay_above_the_road` (Le Mans wall 969, 600 ticks): before,
    body 22 sank 0.099 m at tick 397; after, the deepest is 0.0100 m over 22091 piece-ticks.
