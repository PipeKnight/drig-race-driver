[I] The vtable's object is the one 0x5FAD10 writes.
- Port [deviation, owner request]: `ornament_lod.rs` `shadow_proxies`, `ornament_shadow_fade.wgsl`.
  - Bevy 0.19's shadow pass takes no visibility-range dither, so while an instance crosses D its shadow-level part is `NotShadowCaster` and a proxy casts instead: same mesh and transform, `car_batch::shadow_layer()` (only the sun draws it), `MeshTag` = fade step | appearing << 5.
  - The proxy's material is `ExtendedMaterial<StandardMaterial, ShadowFade>`, a copy of the part's material. Its prepass fragment applies Bevy's 4×4 dither pattern for that step (the same pattern as the colour fade). The copy is a mask material, because Bevy runs a shadow fragment only with `MAY_DISCARD`: opaque becomes `Mask(0)`, alpha-to-coverage `Mask(0.5)`; a blended part keeps the hard switch. One copy per source material, cached.
  - Proxies exist only during a fade. At its end the proxy is despawned and the part casts again; a part that was `NotShadowCaster` before stays so.
  - The material plugin and shader are registered only when a render sub-app exists; the system runs after `select`, before visibility.
  - `GRID_ORNAMENT_FADE=0` keeps the hard switch.
- Test: `a_shadow_crossing_d_casts_through_a_dithered_proxy` (proxy appears with the appearing bit and a rising step, the part stops casting, both are undone at the end).
